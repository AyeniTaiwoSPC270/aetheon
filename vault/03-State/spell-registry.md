## PART 1 — WHAT THE REGISTRY COVERS

Each entry has a TYPE, which decides which rules apply:

| Type | What it is | Uses | Gate rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spell** | A mana-based technique learned and cast by a regular (non-Phasite) mage | Mana channel + circuits | Full Spell Gate (Part 2) |
| **Ki Technique** | A sword/martial technique (e.g. Sever Strike) | Ki, never mana | Ki-title gate (Steel/Iron/Silver/Gold/Obsidian) instead of mage rank; no mana cost; cannot be detected by Mana Sense |
| **Phasite Application** | A named use of a Phasite's unique ability (e.g. Aldric's Pressure Field, Kinetic Reflect, Gravity Spike) | The Phasite's own ability | Not ranked F–S. Gated by the Phasite's Power System Bible entry and progression stage instead (Aldric: Stage 1 uncontrolled → Stage 2 deliberate) |
| **Monster / Non-Mage Ability** | E.g. Beast's Crystal Echo, Beast Resonance | Creature physiology | Follows its own Bible entry; not learnable |

A Spell may **never** replicate a Phasite's concept (Force, Echo, Genesis, Negation, Phase Shift, Second Skin, Wither, Fatethread, Splitform, Magnify, Overdrive). Phasite abilities cannot be learned or copied.

## PART 2 — THE SPELL GATE (mandatory checks)

A spell may appear in a /plan, /write or /rewrite only if it passes **all** of the following. Any failure = do not use it; flag it to the author instead.

**Gate 1 — Registry check.** The spell has a full profile (Part 4 template) in this registry, or a full profile is presented in the current /plan and the author has approved it. No profile = no spell. Never improvise a spell name in prose.

**Gate 2 — Rank check.** The caster's formal rank (F–S) must be **equal to or higher than** the spell's rank. An F-rank cannot cast an S-rank spell, a D cannot cast a C spell, and so on. Phasite Applications and Ki Techniques skip this gate and use their own gates (Part 1).

**Gate 3 — Affinity check.** The spell's element/type must match the caster's affinity. Affinity cannot be changed. Per the rank table: E-rank = single element only; D = reliable single element; C+ = multi-element capable; B+ = complex compound spells. A multi-element spell needs C+; a compound spell needs B+. "Universal" spells (no element) can be cast by any caster who meets the rank gate.

**Gate 4 — Capacity check.** The spell's mana cost must fit inside the caster's *current* mana. Costs are checked against the caster's channel size (Power System Bible Section I) and the exhaustion stages: below 50% = Strain, below 20% = Depletion, below 5% = Critical, empty = Burnout, forced casting = Rupture. Sustained spells cost mana per interval, not once.

**Gate 5 — Circuit check.** The caster's circuit density must meet the spell's Circuit Requirement (complexity, speed, layers). A big channel with poor circuits cannot cast fast or layered spells.

**Gate 6 — Knowledge check.** The spell must be on the caster's **Known Spells list** (Part 5), with *how* and *when* it was learned. A caster may know **any number** of spells, but each must be logged, and the learning must be plausible (academy curriculum year, mentor, self-taught, family tradition). A first-year cannot know an advanced spell unless a source is established on-page or logged in canon.

**Gate 7 — Timeline check.** The spell must be plausible for the saga and the caster's stage. Act 1 rules still apply: Aldric is **magic-only** with no sword training until Act 2; Daran holds at Silver through Saga 5; no Dragonite physical description before its reveal.

**Gate 8 — Consistency check.** Every appearance must match the profile: same effect, same cost, same limits. A spell changes only through a logged **Mastery Upgrade** (author-approved), never silently.

**Gate 9 — Interaction check.** Counters and blind-spots follow the Type Interaction rules (hard counters, soft counters, blindspots). Ki users do not register on Mana Sense; Ki-coated blades disrupt circuits.

**Gate 10 — Name check.** The spell name must not duplicate an existing spell, technique, Phasite ability, or legendary weapon name.

**Gate 11 — Overreach exception (author-only).** A caster attempting a spell above their rank is permitted **only** as an explicit, author-approved story event, and always with a heavy, on-page cost (at minimum Stage 3 exhaustion; circuit damage or rupture possible). It is never a default and never repeatable without rank progression.

## PART 3 — COST SCALE (APPROVED 2026-09-26)

The Power System Bible refers to "standardised mana units" (mu) but gives no numbers. This scale keeps costs consistent. Individual casters vary about ±25% within a rank.

**Reference channel size by mage rank**

| Rank | Reference channel (mu) |
|---|---|
| F | ~30 |
| E | ~80 |
| D | ~200 |
| C | ~500 |
| B | ~1,200 |
| A | ~3,000 |
| S | 8,000+ |

**Spell rank and typical single-cast cost**

| Spell rank | Typical cost per cast (mu) | Minimum caster rank |
|---|---|---|
| F | 2–5 | F |
| E | 5–15 | E |
| D | 15–40 | D |
| C | 40–100 | C |
| B | 100–250 | B |
| A | 250–600 | A |
| S | 600–1,500+ | S |

Each profile states its own exact number. **Sustained spells** list a cost per interval (e.g. per 10 seconds) plus a maximum duration.

**Aldric's channel (approved 2026-09-26):** about 1,200 mu (B-equivalent) from birth — channel size is fixed at birth per the Power System Bible, so it does not grow. What changes across the saga is his control and rank on paper: he registers as D at academy entry (his anomalous reading is filed as unreliable; the academy places him conservatively), and is reassessed B by Saga 3 once he can act deliberately. Percentage costs for his Phasite Applications are percentages of this 1,200.

**Tavish Ardell's channel:** working C-rank (~500 mu reference), consistent with a trained noble first-year with private tutoring since age six.

**Backstage vs on-page:** the numbers above are working notes for consistency. The prose never shows mu, ranks-as-numbers or stat screens (Writing Standards: no game UI). Show consequences instead — fatigue, trembling hands, a spell wavering, a cost the character pays.

**Mana ledger:** /plan lists every spell cast in a chapter with its cost and the caster's running mana total, so exhaustion stages are tracked and enforced.

## PART 7 — HOW SPELLS ENTER CANON

1. Spell is proposed in /plan (or via /suggest) with a full profile.
2. Claude runs the Spell Gate and reports pass/fail for each check.
3. The author approves, edits or rejects.
4. On approval, the profile's status changes to APPROVED and the caster's Known Spells list in Part 5 is updated.
5. /review re-checks every spell used in the chapter against its profile.
6. Any change to an approved spell is a Mastery Upgrade and needs author approval.

## PART 8 — SPELL PROFILES

### 1. MANA BOLT
- TYPE: Spell
- RANK: E
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Universal (no element)
- MULTI-ELEMENT / COMPOUND?: No
- EFFECT: Raw mana compressed into a slug and projected as impact — roughly a hard punch or thrown stone. Stuns or knocks back; does not burn, freeze or cut.
- RANGE / AREA: Single target, about 20 m; loses coherence and force with distance.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: About one second; pointed hand or focus.
- MANA COST: 8 mu per cast.
- CIRCUIT REQUIREMENT: Basic.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: E
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: An unskilled caster scatters the bolt into a wide, weak burst. Weak against any raised barrier.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: Mana Shell absorbs it; wind and distance blunt it; Tideguard banks it (see that profile).
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: Pale unaffiliated blue-white flash and a flat, dry crack.
- TEACHABILITY: Academy, Combat Application, first term.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch. 16 (Bavel's Yard).
- KNOWN BY: All first-years by end of first term. Davan.
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 2. MANA SHELL
- TYPE: Spell
- RANK: D
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Universal
- EFFECT: A hardened mana barrier hugging the caster's body, roughly one arm's length out.
- RANGE / AREA: Self only.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: About one second; brief focus.
- MANA COST: 20 mu to raise, then 4 mu per 10 seconds to hold.
- CIRCUIT REQUIREMENT: Developed.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: D
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: Cannot move outward or be cast on others. Collapses under overload.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: Degrades faster against Ki-coated blades; large sustained force outlasts it.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A faint glassy shimmer at the surface, cracking into lines before it fails.
- TEACHABILITY: Academy, Combat Application, first year.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch. 18 (The Pressure Distortion).
- KNOWN BY: First-years after their first term.
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 3. IGNIS LANCE
- TYPE: Spell
- RANK: D
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Elemental — Fire
- EFFECT: A concentrated beam of fire, thin and fast, that burns through cloth, wood and thin armour.
- RANGE / AREA: Single line, about 15 m.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: About one second for a burst; gesture and focus.
- MANA COST: 30 mu per burst. Holding the beam costs +10 mu per extra second, up to about 3 seconds at D rank.
- CIRCUIT REQUIREMENT: Developed.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: D
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: Loss of control causes flare-back.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: Water suppresses it; wind disperses it; Mana Shell blunts it.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A white-orange line, heat shimmer, the smell of scorched air.
- TEACHABILITY: Academy, Elemental Foundations (fire branch).
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch. 29 (Torval and Rynn).
- KNOWN BY: Rynn (signature, base version).
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 4. FROST LATTICE
- TYPE: Spell
- RANK: C
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Elemental — Ice/Water, with precision control
- EFFECT: Frost spreads from the point of casting, weaving interlaced threads of ice across the ground to slow and bind footing.
- RANGE / AREA: About 6 m radius, spreading over roughly 3 seconds.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: About two seconds; palm to surface or a directed gesture.
- MANA COST: 70 mu per cast; no upkeep.
- CIRCUIT REQUIREMENT: Dense.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: C
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: A hurried cast spreads unevenly.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: Fire melts it fast; Ki-coated blades cut it cleanly.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: White branching crystals, a drop in temperature, breath fog.
- TEACHABILITY: Mentor-taught or advanced curriculum.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch. 36 (Lirien Watching).
- KNOWN BY: Lirien (preferred technique).
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 5. TIDEGUARD
- TYPE: Spell
- RANK: C
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Elemental — Water
- MULTI-ELEMENT / COMPOUND?: No
- EFFECT: Draws ambient moisture into a curved, load-bearing sheet of water anchored in front of the caster. On impact it hardens just enough to take a strike into itself rather than simply blocking it — force is absorbed and "banked" rather than reflected. The caster can release the accumulated load in a single directed counter-wave at a moment of their choosing.
- RANGE / AREA: Self-anchored, roughly a 2 m arc in front of the caster.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: About one second to raise; held via continuous, minimal focus afterward.
- MANA COST: 50 mu to raise; banks absorbed force up to a caster-dependent ceiling (for a C-rank caster, roughly the equivalent of six ordinary Mana Bolts) before it must be released or it fails; releasing the banked load costs a further 25 mu.
- CIRCUIT REQUIREMENT: Developed.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: C
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: The ward can only bank force it can categorize as ordinary kinetic/elemental impact. Held load past its ceiling shatters it outright, releasing everything at once, uncontrolled, in every direction rather than at the caster's choosing.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: Fire evaporates a freshly raised ward before it hardens. A force the ward's structure cannot categorize does not bank cleanly — it either shatters the ward immediately or passes through some of its effect regardless. Confirmed on-page (Ch. 44): a Phasite-conceptual force is exactly such a case, per the Power System Bible's Conceptual Standard — no two Phasites (or Phasite-adjacent forces) share a category with ordinary magic, so the ward has no bucket to sort it into.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A curved sheet of water rising out of the air itself, rippling, then going glassy-still; a growing bank of held force shows as cloudiness stirring inside it, like silt in clear water.
- TEACHABILITY: Mentor-taught; House Ardell family tradition, tutored since age six.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch. 44 (The Final).
- KNOWN BY: Tavish Ardell.
- STATUS: APPROVED 2026-09-28.

### 6. SEVER STRIKE
- TYPE: Ki Technique
- RANK: Silver title minimum (no mage rank; costs no mana)
- ELEMENT / CATEGORY: Ki — martial
- EFFECT: A Ki-coated blade strike aimed at mana circuit pathways. On a clean hit it suppresses mana flow through the struck limb for minutes to hours.
- RANGE / AREA: Contact.
- CAST TIME / METHOD: One committed strike; no words or gesture.
- MANA COST: None. Spends Ki.
- MINIMUM CASTER RANK: Silver (Ki title).
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: Permanent circuit severing is a separate technique, not part of this profile.
- COUNTERS / WEAKNESSES: A well-raised Mana Shell degrades under it.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A faint grey-white sheen along the blade, a numb, dead feeling in the struck limb.
- TEACHABILITY: Ki practitioners at Silver rank and above.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Not yet used on the page. Daran remains below Silver as of Ch. 34.
- KNOWN BY: Daran (from Silver, Saga 4).
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 7. PRESSURE FIELD
- TYPE: Phasite Application (Aldric, Force)
- STAGE GATE: Stage 2 (Saga 3+). Not available in Saga 1.
- EFFECT: A sustained zone of increased pressure/gravity around a point in his line of sight.
- RANGE / AREA: About 3–5 m radius at Stage 2.
- COST: About 6% of his channel (≈72 mu of 1,200) per 10 seconds at a 3 m radius.
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: Chest pain when sustained; overuse leads to bleeding.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A directional silver-white shimmer in the air.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Saga 4 field-test Branded fight (Ch. 51–63).
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 8. KINETIC REFLECT

- COST: Scales with the force redirected — about 3% of his channel for a heavy blow, 10% or more for a Branded-scale strike.
- LIMITS / FAILURE MODE: A mistimed reflect lands on him instead.
- VISUAL / SENSORY SIGNATURE: A pale white-silver flash at the point of contact.
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Saga 4 field-test Branded fight.
- STATUS: APPROVED

### 9. GRAVITY SPIKE
