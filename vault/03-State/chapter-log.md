# **HOW TO USE THIS LOG**

## **The Three-Layer Memory System**

Your project runs on three layers of memory working together:

**Layer 1 — The Bibles** *(permanent world memory)*

Uploaded to your Claude project. Cover all world facts, characters,
power system, lore, and history. Claude reads these at the start of
every session automatically.

**Layer 2 — The Chapter Log** *(recent story memory — this document)*

You maintain this manually. Paste the last 3–5 entries into your message
when starting a new session. Gives Claude recent story context that the
bibles don't cover.

**Layer 3 — The Chapter Character Sheet** *(per-chapter working memory)*

Generated fresh during every /plan phase. Covers exactly who is in the
current chapter, their state, and their role. Expires after each
chapter.

## **The Session Start Formula**

When opening a new Claude session, begin your message like this:

> **Recent chapters:**
>
> *\[Paste last 3–5 chapter log entries here\]*
>
> /plan Chapter \[X\], Arc \[X\], Saga \[X\]. \[Your brief here.\]

## **When to Fill an Entry**

- Immediately after a chapter is approved and finalised — not before.

- Keep each entry honest and specific. 'Things happened' is useless.
  'Daran left without speaking and Aldric noticed' is what Claude needs.

- If a chapter introduces new canon — a new character name, a new
  location, a new established fact — note it here AND run /update-bible
  to add it to the relevant bible.

- Arc Summary entries are filled when an arc concludes — not chapter by
  chapter.

## **What Each Field Means**

**What happened:** 2–3 sentences. The plot events of the chapter. What
changed by the end.

**Character states changed:** Who is different now and how. Emotional
states, relationship shifts, new knowledge.

**New canon introduced:** Any new named character, location, spell, or
established world fact that appeared for the first time.

**Closing beat / hook:** The last image or moment of the chapter. What
it leaves the reader with.
