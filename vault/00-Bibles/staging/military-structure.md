## The Chain of Command (pre-existing, confirmed unchanged)

**King → Noble Council (15 seats, advisory) → Paladins (3, council-appointed, answer directly to the king) → Military Commander → Ranked Mage Units (A–D class, the kingdom's PRIMARY military force) → Sword Units (Ki practitioners — SECONDARY status, a slow-burn resentment thread) → City Guards → Village Guards**

- Mage Units, not Sword Units, are the kingdom's primary military force.
- Paladins sit above the Military Commander in the chain, but their real function is peacetime king's-guard duty and "the kingdom's final answer" in crisis — not day-to-day command of Mage/Sword Units.
- Per this session's Monster Classification System build: **Paladins are specifically the response to Sovereign-tier (Tier 5) threats and above.** Regular Mage/Sword Units handle Tiers 1–4. This is *why* there are only three Paladins — apex-threat response is a narrow, rare skillset the kingdom can't spread thin.

## The Military Commander (new this session)

**Marshal Kaelen Rourke** — an A-class mage, career military, the professional backbone between the Paladins and the actual fighting force. Not cruel or biased by intent — he's perpetuated the Mage-over-Sword status quo through decades of institutional inertia rather than malice. Fits the saga's theme: nobody in this structure is actively villainous, the system just isn't looking hard enough at its own imbalances.

## Unit Organization (new this session)

**Structure model:** A hybrid approach — a small number of elite, named Units (5–10) that matter to the story, sitting atop an unstated broader body of conventional units the story never needs to individually detail.

### Named Sword Units
- **The Ashenguard** — Dessa Rho and Corran Vess's former unit. Its reputation still carries the weight of what happened to them; serving here means inheriting that history whether you want to or not.
- **The Ironline** — Frontier/border-specialized, stationed near Eastern Valdris. Executes Saga 3's child-rescue extraction, given its positioning near where the trouble actually is.

### Named Mage Units
- **The Stormwake** — Rapid-response, elemental specialists, the kingdom's go-to for fast escalation.
- **The Duskwatch** — Investigation and covert operations. Provides the investigative groundwork in Saga 3 that locates where Doven's child is being held, ahead of the Ironline's extraction.

---

## Saga 3 Application

The rescue climax uses both tiers of the military correctly: **the Duskwatch (Mage Unit, primary force) does the investigative groundwork; the Ironline (Sword Unit, secondary force) executes the physical extraction.** This gives both forces a distinct, proportional role rather than treating "the military" as an undifferentiated block — and it's consistent with Mage Units holding primary status while Sword Units carry out the specialized, hands-on operation.
