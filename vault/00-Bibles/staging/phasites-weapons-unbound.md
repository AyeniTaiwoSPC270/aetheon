# Section A — Phasite Standards

Three hard rules now govern every Phasite ability, plus one new standing rule on scope.

**Rule 1 — Conceptual Standard: **A Phasite ability must be conceptually unprecedented — never a stronger or rarer version of an existing spell school (fire, wind, water, light, etc.) or Ki technique. Each of the 11 manipulates a fundamental concept ordinary magic and Ki cannot touch. No two Phasites share a conceptual family.

**Rule 2 — Scale Standard: **Every Phasite ability must have a theoretical true-scale expression capable of kingdom-level or greater impact, physically or strategically. Most Phasites operate at an everyday, controlled level; true scale is rare, costly, or dangerous.

**Rule 3 — Identification: **Standard F-S assessment instruments cannot classify a true Phasite, producing an anomalous reading rather than a clean rank — but this only flags a candidate. True confirmation requires the ability itself to manifest as an unprecedented act.

**NEW — Scattered ****&**** Multi-Race Standing Rule: **Phasites and Legendary Weapon wielders are not confined to Valdenmere or even Valdris — they are scattered across the world. They are not human-exclusive; any race may hold a Phasite ability or wield a Legendary Weapon, EXCEPT goblins, who are explicitly excluded from both.

## 1. Aldric Vane — Force

Manipulates force/pressure directly. Everyday: Pressure Fields. True scale: landmass-level crushing force.

## 2. Serath Voss — Echo

Concept: causality/time. Perceives and forces a very recent moment (seconds) to replay once, altered by a new action taken within it — a localized, self-aware redo. True scale: replaying much larger events (a battle, a day). Cost: his own body, mind, and lifespan.

## 3. Princess Sae Duskwane — Genesis

Concept: willpower given physical form. Creates solid, functional constructs of pure magical energy shaped by will and imagination — not by controlling existing constructs. Everyday: small, simple constructs (a blade, a shield). True scale: massive constructs capable of reshaping a battlefield. Cost: sustaining larger constructs drains willpower and focus intensely; losing concentration can collapse a large construct dangerously. Her Phasite status is known within elven leadership generally, but hidden from Valdenmere and humans entirely — a significant, previously-undefined piece of her flagged future role in the series.

## 4. Kordas — Negation

Concept: cancellation/erasure. Cancels active magical or Ki effects within a radius, reaching into a technique already in motion and switching it off. The old 'Silence Field' (sound suppression) survives only as a minor side-effect, not the core ability. True scale: nullifying all active magic across an entire region simultaneously. Elf. Unbound Core member — loyal to Varek out of belonging, not ideology; feared and isolated his whole life.

## 5. Corin Mercer — Phase Shift

Concept: full space manipulation. Encompasses phasing (intangibility through solid matter), short-range teleportation, creating controlled pocket spaces, and swapping the position of two objects or people instantly. Everyday: phasing, short teleports, small-scale swaps, a modest pocket space. True scale: a full pocket dimension under total control, and swaps at vast scale — trading an army's position with another location, or collapsing a battlefield's geography by force. Cost: physical toll from phasing/teleporting, risk of being lost 'between' if overextended; a maintained pocket space demands continuous exertion, and a broken concentration risks dangerous collapse for anything trapped inside. Human — a thief working Valdris Prime, who despises nobles and the current system.

## 6. Reyus Kade, "The Mirror" — Second Skin

Concept: identity/form. Total physical imprint of one observed person at a time; cannot copy memory or relationships — a built-in detection gap. True scale is strategic rather than physical: a perfect impersonation of the right person reshapes a nation. Currently embedded as 'Lord Cassian Veyra' on the Noble Council (the real Veyra is dead). The only Core member who knows Varek's full plan.

## 7. Ashen Rook — Wither

Concept: entropy/decay. Accelerates decay and aging on contact — rot, rust, spoilage. True scale: could collapse a fortress's structural integrity or wither an entire region's crops, a famine weapon. Cost: using it on living things visibly ages the user's own body in tandem. Demi-human, Central Serath.

## 8. Toren Larke — Fatethread

Concept: probability/fate. Everyday: tilts small outcomes. True scale: kingdom-shaping probability manipulation — tilting a battle, a succession, a harvest. Cost: mental/will erosion; forcing fate at scale costs his own sanity. Backstory: Varek's own research exposed a way to forcibly override a Phasite's will; the crown seized that discovery and weaponized Toren as a state asset. STILL ALIVE, STILL WEAPONIZED — a live plot thread available from Saga 4 onward.

## 9. Juno Vasker — Splitform

Concept: duplication. Creates a genuine, physically real temporary copy of an object or of themselves — not illusion, an actual second instance. True scale: duplicates massive resources or fields an army of real duplicate selves for a short, decisive window. Cost: all copies draw from the same finite source — more copies means each is weaker and the original more drained; overextending risks permanently splitting the original. See Section D for her full Council profile.

## 10. Bram Ostley — Magnify

Concept: scale/size. Shrinks or enlarges objects. True scale: shrinks an army's equipment to uselessness, or magnifies a single strike to city-leveling force. Cost: violent backlash if a large-scale effect breaks. Human, based in Kessari.

## 11. Roan Ashcombe — Overdrive

Concept: self-amplification. Multiplies the user's own existing physical and mental capabilities — strength, speed, reflexes, cognition — by a scalable factor. From Verai (a Valdris-rooted kingdom with a colonial foothold in western Serath); an adventurer currently operating within Valdenmere. Full mechanics in Section C.

# Section C — Overdrive: Full Mechanics

Let n = the order of magnitude of the multiplier (x10 -> n=1, x100 -> n=2, x1,000,000 -> n=6, x1,000,000,000 -> n=9).

**Duration formula: **T = 1 hour x (1/2)^(n-1) — each additional order of magnitude halves the usable duration.

**Life force cost: **5n% of his total reserve, consumed per use.

| **Multiplier** | **n** | **Duration** | **Life Force Cost** | **Combat Benchmark** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| x10 | 1 | 1 hour | 5% | High Silver/Gold-rank swordsman or A-rank mage |
| x100 | 2 | 30 min | 10% | Obsidian-rank Ki master or S-rank mage; Daran near peak |
| x1,000 | 3 | 15 min | 15% | Aldric's peak Force output; a Weapon's everyday capability |
| x10,000 | 4 | 7.5 min | 20% | Contends with a Weapon's true capability briefly |
| x100,000 | 5 | ~3.75 min | 25% | Rivals another Phasite's true-scale ability |
| x1,000,000 | 6 | ~1.9 min | 30% | Can single-handedly turn a large battle |
| x100,000,000 | 8 | ~28 sec | 40% | Reality-straining; body risks tearing itself apart |
| x1,000,000,000 | 9 | ~14 sec | 45% | Momentary godlike output; could briefly contest a Calamity |

# Section D — The Second Council Phasite: Juno Vasker

**Noble house: **House Vasker — one of the 15 old noble seats on Valdenmere's Council.

**Legitimacy: **Her seat is genuinely hers by birth, unlike Reyus Kade's stolen identity as 'Lord Veyra.' This is a deliberate contrast between the Council's two Phasite infiltrators.

**Radicalization: **Having grown up inside the same power structure the Unbound wants dismantled, she witnessed institutional cruelty firsthand from within — closer to guilt than ideology. Privilege she never chose, spent trying to dismantle the system that gave it to her.

**Unbound tier: **Informed — committed, but below the Core. Notably, she is NOT part of the Core despite her position; Reyus Kade remains the only Phasite in Core leadership.

**How the double life works: **Her duplicate is a real, independent instance of herself — the 'Council Juno' and the 'Unbound Juno' can genuinely act and think independently, even disagree, within the limits of Splitform's shared-reserve cost.

# Section F — The Unbound: Sigil, Founding, Funding, Base

**Founding date: **Approximately 16 years before Saga 1 — shortly after Dessa Rho's departure from the Paladins (15-18 years before the story), making her a plausible early Core recruit rather than a much later addition.

**Sigil: **A single broken manacle/shackle link — small, easily concealed, worn as a tattoo or brand rather than displayed. Recognizable only to those who already know to look for it, consistent with a genuinely secret faction.

**Funding model: **Three-layered, matching the Core/Informed/Unaware structure: Juno Vasker's noble house wealth quietly diverted from the top; black-market smuggling and intelligence funneled through Oren Vask's network in the middle; the outer Unaware tier unwittingly providing labor, cover, and small resources, genuinely believing they support reform rather than a faction.

**Physical base: **Hidden in the unclaimed wild land between Corvane, Kessari, and Thalyss — old ruins from the many minor kingdoms that rose and fell in Southern Valdris before Valdenmere's Third Age wars, in a no-man's-land none of the three current kingdoms actually patrol. Kept invisible through Halden Corr's Ward Weaving.
