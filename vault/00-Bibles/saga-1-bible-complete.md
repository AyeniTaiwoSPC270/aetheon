# **PART 1 — SAGA OVERVIEW & STRUCTURE**

Saga 1 runs from Aldric's boyhood in Ashford to the end of his first year at Greyveil Academy. It establishes every relationship, plants every long-arc seed, and closes with the world already moving in ways Aldric does not yet understand. The promise between him and Daran — made in a field at dusk — is the emotional spine. It breaks in Arc 3. Everything after is the consequence of that break.

**Saga 1 closes on Daran. Not Aldric.**
