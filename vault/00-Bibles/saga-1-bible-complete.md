55 Chapters \| 7 Arcs \| Protagonist: Aldric Vane

**PART 1 — SAGA OVERVIEW & STRUCTURE**

Saga 1 runs from Aldric's boyhood in Ashford to the end of his first
year at Greyveil Academy. It establishes every relationship, plants
every long-arc seed, and closes with the world already moving in ways
Aldric does not yet understand. The promise between him and Daran — made
in a field at dusk — is the emotional spine. It breaks in Arc 3.
Everything after is the consequence of that break.

**Saga 1 closes on Daran. Not Aldric.**

**ARC 4 — EARLY ACADEMY ADJUSTMENT**

place it is. The social order is established on day one. First
friendships form. First enemies. The arc closes when his Phasite ability
surfaces unexpectedly in a practical session — Bavel controls it, Maret
documents it, Lirien clocks it from across the room without showing she
did.

place — and what it feels like when that starts to change.*

He is thinking about tomorrow. The saga closes on him. |
