# **SECTION I — PROTAGONISTS**

## **ALDRIC VANE**

**Age:** 15–16 at Saga 1 open; ~25–27 by Saga 8 (per the Saga 2 Bible's
Year-of-the-Fifth-Age timeline and the ~10–12 year series span).

**Classification:** Phasite 1 — Force Manipulation. First dual
sword-and-magic wielder in history. Noble blood, commoner-raised.

**Ability:** Force Manipulation — push/pull, gravity, kinetic
redirection. Full mechanics: Power System Bible, Section V & VI.

**Combat Tier:** Progresses Stage 1 (uncontrolled) → Stage 2 (control) →
Stage 3 (mastery) across the series. By Saga 8, contends at the apex of
both the mage and Ki systems simultaneously — unprecedented.

**Appearance:** White hair, red eyes (silver at full activation). Noble
features that betray his bloodline to anyone who knows what to look for.
Carries himself like someone who grew up working, not like nobility.

**Personality:** Adaptable, observant, reads rooms and people
instinctively. Dry, unexpected humor that appears in the margins of
tension, never at its center. Suppresses and files what he can't process
in the moment — a habit that costs him later.

**Backstory:** Abandoned as an infant in a storage shed on Ashford's
edge; found and raised by Maren and Josse Vane. Grew up alongside Daran
on a shared promise to both become mages. Only he was accepted.

**Relationships:** Daran (the brother he lost), Edrath Solm (first
authority figure who saw him clearly), Rynn & Solen (chosen family),
Lirien (the one person who doesn't treat him as special), Varek Noss
(dark mirror).

**Personal Goal:** Sagas 1–2: belong. Sagas 3–4: protect. Sagas 5–6:
reckon with what he actually believes. Sagas 7–8: accept the crown he
never wanted, because he's the only one every faction trusts.

**Story Role / Arc:** Protagonist and series anchor. Full saga-by-saga
breakdown: character-bible.md, Section I.

**New in this profile:** Estimated age by Saga 8 (proposed — derived
from the Saga 2 Bible's timeline note, not stated outright anywhere
else). Everything else is a reformat of existing canon.

## **DARAN**

**Age:** 15–16 at Saga 1 open, same as Aldric.

**Classification:** Non-mage. Pure swordsman, Forgehall-trained. Finest
in the world by Saga 5.

**Ability:** Ki-based swordsmanship. No magic. Full Blade Title
progression: Power System Bible, Section III.

**Combat Tier:** Steel (end Saga 2) → Iron (Saga 3) → Silver (Saga 4) →
Gold (Saga 5) → Obsidian (Saga 7) — the fastest progression to Obsidian
in recorded history.

**Appearance:** Dark-haired, sharp-featured. Lean, efficient, nothing
wasted — looks carved for combat by adulthood. Eyes carry the specific
coldness of someone who decided warmth was a liability.

**Personality:** Proud, intensely competitive, everything personal and
everything a measure. Buried his childhood warmth under grinding
discipline rather than lost it. Not cruel by nature — decided cruelty
was more useful than grief.

**Backstory:** Commoner, same village as Aldric. Failed the Greyveil
exam (sub-threshold mana channel) and watched Aldric leave without him.
Arrived at Forgehall and clawed his way to Obsidian rank. Kills his
mentor Grevan Tusk in Saga 6 to take the legendary weapon Duskmere — a
permanent, unabsolved scar. Becomes a fugitive after.

**Relationships:** Aldric (lifelong measure, the wound that never fully
closes), Grevan Tusk (the only person who told him the truth, and whom
he killed), Corvin Aske (Saga 3 rival, later shows him mercy as a Sword
Unit officer), Dessa Rho (offered him a shortcut to power in Saga 3; he
refused).

**Personal Goal:** Prove his effort was never the thing that failed —
first through beating Aldric, later (after Saga 6) through carrying a
guilt he never resolves.

**Story Role / Arc:** Wild-card rival. Full saga-by-saga breakdown:
character-bible.md, Section I.

**New in this profile:** Nothing invented — full reformat of existing
(now-updated) canon.

# **SECTION II — ALDRIC'S ACADEMY CIRCLE**

## **RYNN**

**Age:** 15–16, Aldric's year.

**Classification:** Commoner. Fire-adjacent mage.

**Ability:** Fire magic with a physical-enhancement component. Output
runs hot and instinctive — hard to control, mirrors his personality.

**Combat Tier:** Not yet formally ranked as of Ch. 13 (still Foundation
year); by the war sagas a genuinely formidable fighter per his existing
arc summary.

**Appearance:** *Proposed — not yet established.* Reads as
physically warm/flushed easily (per Ch. 13's "running warm from the
session"), a restless quality even at rest — someone who telegraphs his
next move before he's decided to make it.

**Personality:** Loud, hotheaded, fights first and apologizes too
loudly after. Deeply, almost embarrassingly loyal underneath the
bravado — would die for Aldric without thinking about it.

**Backstory:** Commoner-born, shared outsider status is what bonds him
to Aldric immediately. First on-page act: crossing the common room in
three steps to hit Crest Halvon on Aldric's behalf before they'd
exchanged ten words (Ch. 13).

**Relationships:** Aldric (first academy friend, instant and
unplanned), Solen (opposite who needs him), Torval (Arc 5 fight ends in
mutual respect), Davan (Arc 5 practical-session incident together).

**Personal Goal:** To matter to the people he's chosen, loudly and
without hedging.

**Story Role / Arc:** Comic energy in early sagas, grows into someone
who learns loyalty requires wisdom as well as courage. Saga 8 moment
earned per character-bible.md.

**New in this profile:** Appearance details (proposed, inferred from
Ch. 13's physical description of him "running warm").

**Age:** 15–16, Aldric's year.

**Classification:** Minor noble. Precision / circuit-threading mage.

**Ability:** Circuit Threading — directs mana through specific pathways
for precision effects. Not the most powerful mage in the group; the
most controlled.

**Combat Tier:** *Proposed — not yet ranked on-page;* elects the

intelligent of the group; reads Aldric better than Aldric reads
himself.

**Backstory:** Minor noble family — enough to understand noble society

**Personal Goal:** To use what he notices before it's too late to
matter.

**Story Role / Arc:** Becomes the group's strategist in the war sagas;
his noble background gives him political access. Saga 2 gives him a
dedicated POV chapter (Ch. 39, "What Solen Does With Anger").

**New in this profile:** Appearance and pre-Saga-1 combat rank
(unestablished, flagged rather than invented).

# **SECTION V — THE VILLAIN & THE UNBOUND CORE**

## **MASTER VAREK NOSS**

**Age:** Late 50s.

**Classification:** Former Phasite researcher. Leader of the Unbound.
A-rank mage (pre-transformation), Conceptual affinity.

**Ability:** Latticework — perceives and maps the structure of mana
itself. Full mechanics: Power System Bible, Section IX.

**Combat Tier:** Beats a Paladin through analysis, not raw output. Later

agrees with someone.

**Backstory:** Decades of Phasite research revealed the 11 exist to

force the Phasites' purpose, and — privately — achieve Dragonite
transformation. Regards Ki users with intellectual dismissiveness; Dessa
Rho is his one exception.

**Relationships:** Reyus Kade (the only Core member who knows his full
plan — genuine partnership), Dessa Rho (respected exception to his view
of Ki), Vampirica (raised her).

**Personal Goal:** See Backstory — three layers, the last one entirely
his own.

**Story Role / Arc:** Primary antagonist. Full arc: character-bible.md,
