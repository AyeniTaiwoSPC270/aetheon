# **SECTION I — THE HISTORY OF AETHON**

Aethon's history is divided into five named eras. Most ordinary humans in Valdenmere know the last two eras in rough outline. Elven scholars know all five in detail. Demi-human oral traditions describe the same events from perspectives that differ significantly from the written human record.

*Writing rule: Characters reference history naturally — through idioms, offhand comparisons, and assumptions. Let historical references breathe. Explain only when a character would genuinely explain.*

## »» THE FOUNDING MYTH (merged 2026-09-26 from NEW_CANON_HISTORY_OF_AETHON.md — full detail, supersedes the shorter First Age paragraph below)

In the unrecorded First Age, the Dragonites' own essence — blood, breath, being — given form, became magic itself. This is the literal, in-world origin of all magic in Aethon.

In this same era, **Vherith, the Hollow King**, a great demon, rose as a world-ending threat. **The 7 Great Powers** — one champion each from Dragonite, elf, demi-human, goblin, human, demon, and angel — fought and **sealed** him (not killed). The sealing drained them to the edge of their own existence. Rather than fade immediately, they used what remained of themselves to create an ongoing safeguard against future threats of Vherith's kind: **the 11 Phasites** — deliberately distinct in number and nature from the 7 Weapons/Powers themselves. That final act of creation finished what the sealing began. **The 7 Great Powers ceased to exist**, leaving only the **7 Legendary Weapons** behind as their legacy (see Character Bible, Section V-C).

**The human seat:** humans, though the youngest and least-established people at the time, still held one of the 7 seats — one exceptional individual, Edrin Halloway, not a civilizational achievement. An early thematic echo of the series' own throughline.

**The demon and angel seats:** Vherith was opposed by a coalition that included one of his own kind (the demon Kaszrath the Broken Horn) standing beside an angel (Auralei). Aethon's demon/angel dynamic is NOT a simple good/evil split — Act 3 territory, not to be developed further without author instruction.

**Public knowledge — the Dual Path's true origin:** This myth is publicly known and survives as the literal foundation of the Dual Path religion (Section III): **the Forge** tradition venerates the 7 Great Powers, who forged Vherith's seal through Ki/martial sacrifice; **the Current** tradition venerates the Dragonites, whose essence flows through the world as magic. This reframes the Obsidian Compact (Third Age) as a political formalization of a principle the religion had already held sacred for millennia.

**Why Dragonites are distant today:** with Vherith sealed and the Phasites created, the surviving Dragonites' purpose was fulfilled and the greatest among them were already spent. They withdrew into deliberate distance, choosing to watch rather than interfere.

**Status of the seal:** fully stable. Not a live plot thread — a long-game hook only.

## »» FIRST AGE SOCIETIES (pre-kingdom)

- **Elves:** Long-lived observers and record-keepers even in the First Age — the reason elven scholars alone know all five Ages in full detail, and a trait that foreshadows their Third Age complicity (see below).
- **Demi-humans:** Warrior/clan-based societies, organized around strength, honor, and territory from the very start.
- **Goblins:** Small, scattered clans. No unity yet — the Grath Confederacy is a much later achievement.
- **Humans:** Arrived last. The youngest race, least established — despite one individual holding a seat among the 7 Great Powers.

**THE FIRST AGE — The Age of Roots** *(Unknown duration — before recorded history)*

The period before organised kingdoms. Dragonites moved freely across all three continents. Magic was not classified or ranked. No written records exist from this period besides elven oral history. This is also the era of the Founding Myth above — Vherith's sealing and the Phasites' creation.

**THE SECOND AGE — The Age of Ordering** *(Approximately 800–1200 years before the story)*

Humans began building the first organised kingdoms on Valdris, driven by ambition — a hunger for power and territory the older races weren't pursuing the same way. Their edge: the scholar **Drevos** codified the mana channel/circuit system, turning magic from an instinctive, tribally-taught gift into a scalable, organizable resource — the direct enabling technology behind human kingdoms rising faster and harder than any other people. The first inter-racial treaties were signed. **Demi-human territory in eastern Valdris was formally recognised — but only after early human expansion attempts were fought to a military standstill by demi-human warrior-clans.** Recognition was a concession born of respect for their strength, not generosity. Goblins had a functioning nation — the Grath Confederacy — occupying southern Valdris. Human expansion, fueled by that same ambition, pushed into Grath Confederacy territory: **humans are the clear aggressor** in the resulting first human-goblin war, though later human record mythologizes it as "mutual" or "disputed." This conflict is the direct template for the Burning to come.

**THE THIRD AGE — The Age of Conflict** *(Approximately 400–800 years before the story)*

The great wars. Human kingdoms rose and consumed each other — the pattern from the first human-goblin conflict repeats, catastrophically. The Grath Confederacy was destroyed in what human records call the Southern Pacification and goblin oral tradition calls **the Burning** — a **coordinated magical scorched-earth campaign**: human kingdoms, fielding mage armies at a scale only possible because of Drevos's systematized magic, deliberately burned Grath Confederacy lands and cities as policy. The very tool that let humans organize became the instrument of the atrocity. Led, per goblin oral tradition, in part by the resistance of **Skarn Emberkin**, whose stand is the origin point of the Ember spiritual tradition (see Section III). The original peace treaty gave goblins protected status; it has since been rewritten twice. **The elves, witnessing the brutality of the Burning, withdrew from human politics out of moral horror — and this withdrawal is genuine complicity, not neutrality.** They could have intervened and chose not to. Their isolationist faction still defends the choice as correct; others do not. This directly informs Elyn Dawnveil and the later elf POV material. The Obsidian Compact was signed at the end of this age — sword and magic traditions formally declared co-equal, now understood as a political echo of the much older Dual Path religious framework.

**THE FOURTH AGE — The Age of Institutions** *(Approximately 100–400 years before the story)*

The building period. The Greyveil Academy was founded 340 years before the story begins by seven A-rank mages, led by one clear driving figure among them, on a personal, apolitical belief: raw talent was being wasted without structure. The modern F-to-S mage ranking system was formally adopted. The goblin indentured servitude system was legally codified into slavery — the institutional hardening of what the Burning started. Demi-human eastern land, once won through demonstrated strength, began eroding again — this time through pure demographic pressure, slow impersonal population growth pushing human settlers outward (the direct root of Saga 2's border conflict).

**Varek Noss — full origin:** At the age's end, Varek Noss is born. Before any organization exists, he finds and **raises Vampirica** — recognizing what she is from his own research — the first true bond of his life, predating any ideology. Later, employed and funded by Valdenmere's own institutions, his Phasite research uncovers the deep truth: the Calamities, the 7 Great Powers, the real reason the Phasites exist. The discovery comes at a personal cost — his own methods expose a way to **forcibly override a Phasite's ability against their will**. The crown/academy seizes this discovery and uses it to capture and weaponize the Phasite he was studying — **Toren Larke** (Phasite 8) — as a state asset. Varek's faith in the institutions he served breaks at the exact moment he learns something enormous and true. This double wound — love for someone the world would hunt for existing, and guilt/betrayal over what his own research enabled — becomes the true foundation of the Unbound. Not ideology first. The people first. **The Core of 7 comes together** through a mix of Varek's direct recruitment (people like Kordas, isolated and feared his whole life, given a place to belong for the first time) and organic growth through the network (Halden Corr, a former research peer, helping find others).

**THE FIFTH AGE — The Age of the Story** *(The present)*

The age in which the story takes place — now understood to span the full 4-Act, ~20-saga structure rather than a flat 8 sagas. It begins without a name. Elven historians have begun calling it the Age of Correction in their private records, referencing the Phasite prophecy.

## **Key Historical Events Referenced in the Story**

**The Burning** — The destruction of the Grath Confederacy approximately 300 years before the story. Human records call it the Southern Pacification. Sera's liberation movement invokes it as their foundational historical injustice. Cross-reference: **Skarn Emberkin** (Section IV-A), whose resistance during the Burning is the root of the Ember tradition.

# **SECTION II — MAGICAL TERMINOLOGY & NAMED SPELLS**

*(Unchanged from v1.0 except where noted. See Power System Bible for the authoritative, fully mechanical version of all Aldric techniques and Phasite abilities.)*

## **Core Magical Terminology**

**Mana Weave** — The process of shaping raw mana output into a structured spell.

**Circuit Burn** — Overuse injury to mana circuits. Aldric experiences this regularly. »» *Note: with Aldric's sword training now deferred to Act 2+, his Circuit Burn references in Act 1 are from pure magical overexertion, not dual-use — dual-use circuit strain becomes relevant only from Act 2 onward.*

**Channel Depth** — Measured capacity of a mage's mana channel.

**Mana Signature** — Unique pattern of a mage's mana output. Phasites have signatures structurally unlike any regular mage's.

**Circuit Threading** — Directing mana through specific circuit pathways for precision effects. Solen's primary method.

**Void Casting** — Casting while in mana Stage 3 or beyond.

**Ki Bloom** — Visible manifestation of concentrated Ki output.

**Resonance Break** — Mana output accidentally synchronising with an object's natural frequency.

**Force Lattice** — Aldric's coined term for his sustained force-structure field. Appears in Varek's research notes by later Act 1/Act 2.

## **Named Spells — Common Academy Curriculum**

**Mana Bolt** *[Universal Offensive]* — Raw mana compressed and projected as impact.

**Mana Shell** *[Universal Defensive]* — Hardened mana barrier around the caster.

**Ignis Lance** *[Fire — Offensive]* — Concentrated fire beam. Rynn's signature.

**Frost Lattice** *[Ice/Water — Area Control]* — Spreading ice/frost. Lirien's preferred technique.

**Sever Strike** *[Ki — Blade Technique]* — Ki-enhanced strike directed at circuit pathways. Requires Silver-level Ki minimum.

**Pressure Field** *[Force — Aldric's Technique]* — Sustained zone of increased gravitational pressure. Used in the Saga 4 field-test Branded fight.

**Kinetic Reflect** *[Force — Aldric's Technique]* — Redirects incoming kinetic energy back at its source. Used in the Saga 4 field-test Branded fight.

**Gravity Spike** *[Force — Aldric's Technique, Stage 2+]* — A concentrated point of massively increased gravity. Used in the Saga 4 field-test Branded fight.

# **SECTION III — THE RELIGIOUS FRAMEWORK OF AETHON**

*(Unchanged in substance from v1.0.)*

## **The Dual Path — Human Dominant Faith**

The Forge (physical mastery, the sword) and the Current (magical flow, the arcane) as equal, sacred forces. Clergy are called Walkers. Practitioners swear oaths "by Forge and Current."

## **The Ember — Goblin Spiritual Tradition**

The Ember is the irreducible self that persists through suffering if it refuses to be extinguished. Central ritual: **the Telling**. To "lose the Ember" is the deepest goblin spiritual failure. »» **Root cross-reference (2026-09-26):** the tradition traces to **Skarn Emberkin**'s resistance during the Burning (Third Age) — see Section IV-A. Battle cry: "**Gratha**" — "the Ember holds."

## **The Long Memory — Elven Spiritual Tradition**

Ancestral reverence combined with the belief that significant events leave a permanent impression on the world's fabric, read by trained **Readers**. The Phasite prophecy is a Reading.
