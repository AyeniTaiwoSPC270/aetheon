# **SECTION I — PROTAGONISTS**

**ALDRIC VANE***  —  The Protagonist — The First of His Kind*

## **Core Identity**

**Full Name: **Aldric Vane

**Age at Saga 1: **15–16

**Appearance: **White hair, red eyes. Noble features that betray his bloodline to anyone who knows what to look for. Carries himself like someone who grew up working — not stiff, not ceremonial.

**Blood: **Noble — lineage unknown to him. Abandoned as an infant and raised by commoners.

**Classification: **Phasite — one of only 11 in existence. His specific ability is documented in the Power System Bible.

**Distinction: **The first person in recorded history to wield both magic and the sword simultaneously. »» NOTE (2026-09-26): sword training does not begin until Act 2 (Saga 6) — see Power System Bible Section VI. All of Act 1 is magic-only for him.

## **Personality**

Aldric is fundamentally adaptable — he reads rooms, reads people, and adjusts instinctively. He survived being an outsider at the academy not by fighting every battle but by knowing which ones mattered. He is observant in ways people don't expect, which means he often surprises them. He also has a streak of genuine silliness — dry humor, unexpected jokes at tense moments, the ability to make someone laugh when they desperately need it. This is not a mask. It is as real as his intensity.

*Writing note: Aldric's humor should never undercut serious moments — it appears in the margins of tension, not at its centre. It makes him feel human rather than legendary. Even kings can be ridiculous sometimes.*

## **Internal Wound**

Aldric's deepest fear is losing the people he has chosen as family. He has no blood family he knows. His adoptive parents, his two friends, his love interest — these people are everything. The idea of losing them the way the principal was lost in »» Saga 5 (amended 2026-09-26, previously Saga 4) is the thing that genuinely breaks him. Every major sacrifice in the story cuts deeper because of this.

His secondary fear — which he does not consciously name — is that his power makes him something other people cannot fully reach. That the Phasite in him is a wall between him and the ordinary life he sometimes aches for.

## **Motivation Arc — Across the Series**

**Act 1 (Sagas 1-5): **Proves himself, then protects. The academy is a world that rejects him by default and he refuses to be rejected; then the people around him start dying and being betrayed, sharpening his motivation from ambition into something fiercer and more personal.

**Act 2+: **Reckons, then accepts. The world is bigger and uglier than the academy ever prepared him for — he starts asking what he actually believes, and eventually accepts what the world needs him to be, not for glory but because he is the only one every faction will follow.

## **Relationships**

**Daran: **The brother he lost. The wound that never fully closes. Their final handshake in the series' endgame is the emotional resolution of the entire series.

**The Principal: **The first authority figure who saw him without prejudice. His death (»» Saga 5) is the moment Aldric stops being a student in his soul. »» NOTE (2026-09-28): his first full on-page scene and first POV chapter, and Aldric's first physical intervention on his behalf, land in Saga 1 Arc 6 (Ch. 45–46) — see Section III below.

**His Two Friends: **His chosen family at the academy. He is protective of them to a fault.

**The Love Interest: **She challenges him in ways no one else does. He falls for her precisely because she does not treat him as special.

**The Faction Boss: **A dark mirror — someone who also saw the world's rot clearly, and chose destruction instead of endurance.

**DARAN***  —  The Rival — The Greatest Sword in the World*

## **Core Identity**

**Full Name: **Daran (family name deliberately withheld — he stopped using it)

**Age at Saga 1: **15–16 — same as Aldric

**Appearance: **Dark-haired, sharp-featured. He grew into someone who looks like he was carved for combat — lean, efficient, nothing wasted. His eyes carry the specific coldness of someone who decided warmth was a liability.

**Blood: **Commoner — same background as Aldric, which makes the divergence between them even more pointed.

**Classification: **Non-mage. Pure swordsman. The finest in the world by »» later in the series (exact saga TBD, no longer tied to Saga 5 — see rank progression below).

## **Personality**

Proud and intensely competitive — for Daran, everything is personal and everything is a measure. He does not do anything halfway. The warmth he had as a child was not destroyed; it was methodically buried under years of grinding, suffering, and choosing not to feel what hurt. He is not cruel by nature. He is someone who decided cruelty was more useful than grief.

*Writing note: Daran should never feel like a simple villain. His pride is earned — he built himself from nothing through sheer will. The tragedy is that Aldric did the same thing and they ended up on opposite sides of it.*

## **The Core Wound**

As children, Aldric had natural gifts. Daran had only will. He was always the harder worker, always the one who had to try twice as much to keep up. He did not resent this — until the academy exam. When the mana assessment showed he lacked the channel capacity to be a mage, it was not just a rejection. It was the world confirming his worst belief about himself: that his effort was never going to be enough.

The broken promise — 'we will both become mages' — is the wound underneath all the hatred. He did not hate Aldric for getting in. He hated Aldric for making a promise he could not keep.

## **The Convincing**

By the time Daran reappears as an antagonist he has genuinely convinced himself he feels nothing for Aldric. Not hatred — nothing. He has reclassified their friendship as a childhood error. This is the lie he lives in until »» Act 2, when he witnesses the faction's true nature and something in him — something he thought he had buried — hesitates. »» AMENDED (2026-09-26): this crack is now caused directly by killing Grevan Tusk (see below), not by the faction alone. The crack surprises him more than anyone.
