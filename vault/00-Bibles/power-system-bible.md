# **SECTION I — THE MANA FRAMEWORK**

Magic in this world operates through a biological system present in
every mage from birth. It has two core components — the Mana Channel and
the Mana Circuits. Together they determine what a mage can do, how much
they can do, and what happens when they push too far.

## **The Mana Channel**

The Mana Channel is an internal reservoir located at the core of a
mage's body — roughly the chest and abdomen. It stores raw mana. Think
of it as a tank. Its size is determined at birth and cannot be
fundamentally enlarged through training alone, though it can be
conditioned to fill more efficiently and recover faster.

- A larger channel means more mana available before exhaustion.

- Channel size is one of the primary factors measured in a mage's class
  ranking.

- A completely depleted channel causes unconsciousness and, in extreme
  cases, permanent damage.

- Mana regenerates naturally through rest, food, and certain herbs. Rate
  varies by individual.

## **Mana Circuits**

Mana Circuits are the network of pathways running throughout a mage's
body — from their channel outward through their limbs, spine, and
extremities. They allow mana to flow from the channel to where it is
needed for casting. Think of them as veins, but for magical energy.

- Circuit density determines how fast and how complexly a mage can cast.

- More circuits allow multi-layered spells, faster output, and finer
  control.

- Circuits can be damaged through overuse, trauma, or external attack.

- CRITICAL: If a mage's circuits are severed — through a specific
  cutting technique or certain magical attacks — they lose access to
  their magic. This loss may be temporary or permanent depending on
  severity.

- Circuit severing is considered one of the most brutal techniques in
  combat. Its use carries social and legal weight in most kingdoms.

> *Writing note: Circuit severing should be treated as the magical
> equivalent of a crippling injury. When it happens in the story it must
> carry full dramatic weight. Do not use it casually.*

## **Mana Exhaustion — Stages**

**Stage 1 — Strain:** Mana below 50%. Mild fatigue. Casting begins to
feel effortful. No visible symptoms.

**Stage 2 — Depletion:** Mana below 20%. Noticeable physical weakness.
Spells begin to destabilise. Hands may tremble.

**Stage 3 — Critical:** Mana below 5%. Severe pain in the channel and
circuits. Spells may misfire or collapse. Collapse risk.

**Stage 4 — Burnout:** Channel fully empty. Unconsciousness. Potential
circuit damage. Recovery time: days to weeks.

**Stage 5 — Rupture:** Forced casting past burnout. Channel and circuits
physically rupture. Permanent damage likely. Potentially fatal.

## **The Mana Sense**

Trained mages develop a passive ability called Mana Sense — the ability
to feel the presence and rough output level of mana in their
environment. Higher ranked mages have more refined Mana Sense. S-class
mages can detect mana signatures at significant distances and identify
individuals by their mana pattern. Phasites have an instinctive Mana
Sense from birth that surpasses even S-class mages.

# **SECTION II — THE MAGE RANK SYSTEM**

All magic users in this world are formally classified into seven ranks —
F through S. Rank is determined through a combination of four factors
measured at official assessment centres run by the kingdoms. Ranks are
not permanent — a mage can be reassessed and promoted. Demotion is
theoretically possible but extremely rare.

## **The Four Assessment Criteria**

- Mana Channel Capacity — raw size of the internal reservoir, measured
  in standardised mana units.

- Circuit Density & Quality — number, distribution, and condition of
  mana circuits.

- Output Force — maximum mana release in a single burst, measured
  against standardised targets.

- Skill & Technique — practical combat or application assessment. A mage
  who scores high on raw power but cannot control it will be ranked
  lower. Precision, creativity, and adaptability are all evaluated.

> *No single criterion determines rank. A mage with a massive channel
> but poor circuits and no technique may rank lower than a mage with a
> smaller channel and exceptional control. The system rewards the
> complete mage.*

## **Rank in Society**

- F and E ranked individuals are not considered mages in formal contexts
  — they are 'mana-sensitive'.

- D rank is the minimum for academy graduation and professional mage
  certification.

- B rank and above receive significant social privileges in most
  kingdoms — stipends, noble recognition, legal protections.

- A rank mages are treated as national assets. Some kingdoms require
  them to register and serve.

- S rank mages are effectively political entities. They are courted,
  feared, and occasionally assassinated.

- Aldric's rank at various stages: enters the academy registering as D
  (his Phasite nature confuses initial assessment) → reassessed as B by
  Saga 3 → Saga 5 onwards his rank becomes politically contested because
  standard instruments cannot fully measure him.

# **SECTION III — KI & THE SWORD USER SYSTEM**

Not all combat practitioners use mana. Sword users — and certain martial
specialists — operate on an entirely different energy system called Ki.
Ki and mana are distinct forces. They do not interact cleanly, which is
part of what makes a skilled sword user uniquely dangerous to mages.

## **What Is Ki**

Ki is the body's own refined life force — drawn from physical
conditioning, breath control, and the perfection of technique over years
of training. Unlike mana, Ki is not stored in a channel. It is generated
continuously by the body and accumulated through disciplined practice. A
Ki user who stops training loses potency. A mage's mana channel is fixed
at birth. A Ki user's ceiling is defined entirely by their dedication.

- Ki users have minimal to no mana. Daran's mana channel was assessed as
  sub-threshold at the academy exam — this is standard for naturally
  Ki-inclined individuals.

- Ki amplifies physical abilities — speed, strength, reflexes, and blade
  sharpness — beyond what an unenhanced body can achieve.

- Advanced Ki users can coat their blade in concentrated Ki, allowing it
  to cut through magical barriers and interfere with mana circuits
  directly.

- Ki cannot cast spells. It has no elemental affinity. It is force,
  precision, and will made physical.

## **Ki Ranks — The Blade Titles**

Sword users do not use the F-to-S mage classification. They have their
own recognition system — not numerical ranks but titles earned through
combat reputation, formal duels, and peer acknowledgment. The titles
from lowest to highest are:

**Steel:** Entry level — a competent sword user who has demonstrated
reliable Ki control.

**Iron:** Skilled practitioner. Can trade blows with C-rank mages.
Respected in military contexts.

**Silver:** Elite sword user. Ki output is refined enough to coat the
blade. Dangerous to B-rank mages.

**Gold:** Master level. Rare. Can threaten A-rank mages in direct
combat. National-level reputation.

**Obsidian:** The apex title. Fewer than 10 confirmed at any time in
history. Their blade Ki can sever mana circuits. Daran reaches this by
Saga 7.

> *Daran's progression: Steel by end of Saga 2. Iron by Saga 3. Silver
> by Saga 4. Gold by Saga 5 (the draw with Aldric). Obsidian by Saga 7.
> He is the fastest progression to Obsidian in recorded history.*

## **Mana vs Ki — Combat Interactions**

- Ki-coated blades can physically disrupt mana circuits on contact —
  making skilled sword users uniquely terrifying to mages.

- Mana-based barriers are partially effective against Ki attacks but
  degrade faster than against magic. Ki cuts through magical defences
  differently to how spells do.

- A sword user cannot be detected by Mana Sense — they produce no mana
  signature. This makes them invisible to a mage's most reliable
  early-warning system.

- At high levels, concentrated Ki strikes can temporarily suppress a
  mage's circuit flow in a localised area — stunning their ability to
  cast from that limb.

- Aldric is the only practitioner who operates in both systems
  simultaneously — his Phasite force manipulation is mana-based while
  his swordsmanship is Ki-enhanced. This combination has never been
  recorded before him.

# **SECTION IV — MAGIC TYPES & INTERACTIONS**

Regular mages — non-Phasites — express magic through elemental or
conceptual affinities. These affinities are not chosen. They emerge
naturally as a mage's circuits develop, usually in childhood. A mage
cannot change their affinity but can learn to use it in increasingly
sophisticated ways.

## **Primary Magic Categories**

**Elemental:** Fire, water, earth, wind, lightning. The most common
affinity types. Widely studied, well-documented, and the basis of most
combat magic taught at academies.

**Physical Enhancement:** Mana channelled into the body — strength,
speed, durability. Not as flashy as elemental magic but exceptionally
dangerous in close combat. Rynn's fire magic has an enhancement
component.

**Precision / Technical:** Mana expressed through fine control rather
than raw output. Solen's affinity. Allows complex spell construction,
mana surgery, and barrier work of exceptional quality.

**Sensory:** Mana used for detection, tracking, and information
gathering. Mana Sense is a basic version of this. Full sensory mages are
rare and invaluable for intelligence work.

**Conceptual:** Rare. Magic that operates on abstract principles rather
than physical elements — time perception, spatial warping, probability.
These mages often confuse assessors and are poorly understood.

**Phasite:** Categorically different from all above. See Section V.

## **Type Interactions — How Magic Counters Magic**

Magic type interactions exist on three levels:

- HARD COUNTERS: Certain types have direct physical advantages. Water
  suppresses fire. Earth absorbs lightning. Wind disperses fog and
  smoke-based magic. These counters are consistent and taught at
  academies.

- SOFT COUNTERS: Some interactions depend on skill level and technique.
  A skilled fire mage can overcome a water mage of lower rank by sheer
  output. Type disadvantage can be overcome with sufficient mastery.

- TYPE BLINDSPOTS: Certain magic types simply do not interact cleanly.
  Precision magic and raw elemental magic operate on different
  frequencies — a precision mage cannot easily counter elemental brute
  force, but can outmanoeuvre it. Conceptual magic frequently bypasses
  elemental interactions entirely.

> *Aldric's force manipulation does not fit neatly into any counter
> system. Force affects the physical world directly — it can redirect
> fire, crush water constructs, and disrupt earth formations. But it has
> no elemental affinity to counter. This is part of what makes Phasites
> strategically terrifying.*

At the Greyveil entrance exam practical stage, Aldric's output caused
the glass indicator column to climb past the instrument's midpoint and
continue. The examining faculty member performed a calibration check
mid-reading, made an extended notation, and moved him through without
comment. This is the second recorded instance of the institutional
response to Aldric's output — the first being the preliminary assessor's
double-check in Chapter 3. The formal notation now exists in Greyveil's
assessment records. Its contents are unknown to Aldric. Pattern
established: the instrument does not fail or break — it registers
correctly, and the result is what is anomalous.

Training construct — combat stage:

An enchanted practice form in roughly human torso shape, mounted on a
weighted post. Surface is dull iron-grey, brightens and darkens when
active — darkness indicates maximum resistance output. Adapts its
resistance to the candidate's output level, finds their ceiling, and
tests against it. Scored on accuracy, output efficiency, and
adaptability. Cannot be deceived by technique alone — it responds to
actual output. Daran's performance in Chapter 9 drove the construct to
its darkest recorded resistance level during that session. The faculty
examiners stopped writing briefly during his assessment.

Daran — sub-threshold result confirmed (Chapter 9):

Daran's mana channel assessed as sub-threshold at the formal Greyveil
entrance exam. Consistent with Power System Bible canon for Ki-inclined
individuals. His combat stage performance — the strongest physical
assessment of any candidate present — could not compensate for the
channel result under the four-criteria assessment system. This result is
now formal kingdom record.

# **SECTION V — THE 11 PHASITES**

> *There are exactly 11 Phasites in the world. This number is fixed and
> absolute. Do not create additional Phasites. Do not alter established
> Phasite abilities without author instruction.*

## **What Makes a Phasite**

- A Phasite is a mage whose magical ability is entirely unique — the
  first of its kind ever recorded.

- Their ability did not exist in any documented form before them. It
  cannot be learned, copied, or inherited.

- Phasites register anomalously on standard mana assessment instruments
  — some read as impossibly high, some read as broken equipment.

- All Phasites have mana channels and circuits, but their architecture
  is different from standard mages — more complex, with circuit patterns
  that assessors have never seen before.

- Phasites are not more powerful than S-class mages in raw output terms.
  They are categorically different — their abilities operate on
  principles that standard magic theory cannot fully explain.

- Varek Noss's research established that the 11 Phasites are not random
  anomalies. They are the world's response to something. What that
  something is becomes the central mystery of Sagas 6–8.

## **The 11 Phasites — Full Roster**

**PHASITE 1 — ALDRIC VANE**

**Ability Name / Type:** Force Manipulation — the ability to control
push/pull forces, gravity, and kinetic energy. The first and only mage
in history to also wield the sword at Phasite level.

**Visual Signature:** When Aldric's force manipulation activates, the
air around him visibly distorts — a faint silver-white shimmer, like
heat haze but colder. Objects affected by his force glow briefly with a
pale white-silver light at the point of contact. In full activation his
eyes shift from red to a deep luminous silver. The effect is subtle at
low power and increasingly dramatic at high output.

**Cost & Limits:** Line of sight required for all force effects. The
further the target, the greater the mana cost. Using force manipulation
and swordsmanship simultaneously strains his circuits — time limited in
early sagas, painful throughout. Overuse causes circuit burning —
physical pain radiating from the chest outward. Stage 3 overuse causes
visible bleeding from the nose and eyes. Burnout collapses both his mana
and his physical capacity simultaneously.

**First Appearance:** Saga 1 — awakening

**Story Role:** Protagonist. The anchor of the entire series. His dual
nature is the story's central impossibility.

**PHASITE 2 — SERATH VOSS**

**Ability Name / Type:** Echo Magic — the ability to replay any magical
or physical event he has witnessed, manifesting it as a real occurrence
up to three times. If he watched a building collapse, he can collapse it
again. If he witnessed a powerful spell, he can echo it — but at the
cost of his own mana, not the original caster's.

**Visual Signature:** When Serath activates an Echo, a translucent
golden afterimage of the original event appears first — like a ghost of
the moment — then snaps into reality. His eyes turn entirely gold during
activation. The effect lasts only as long as the original event did.

**Cost & Limits:** He can only echo events he personally witnessed. He
cannot echo Phasite abilities — they are too structurally foreign to
replicate. Each echo costs significant mana. He can store up to three
echoes at once — using a fourth forces the oldest to dissolve. He cannot
echo the same event twice.

**First Appearance:** Saga 6

**Story Role:** Ally — one of the first Phasites Aldric encounters. His
ability makes him invaluable in intelligence and ambush scenarios. His
personality is methodical and slightly unsettling.

**PHASITE 3 — MIRA SOLH**

**Ability Name / Type:** Wound Transfer — the ability to move injuries,
pain, or physical damage from one person to another, including to
herself. She can heal anyone by absorbing their damage into her own
body, or transfer a wound from herself to an unwilling target.

**Visual Signature:** Her hands glow a deep crimson when transferring
wounds — the colour darkens the more severe the injury. Transferred
wounds manifest on the recipient identically to how they appeared on the
donor. She leaves no visible sign on her body until the transfer is
complete — then the marks appear instantly.

**Cost & Limits:** She cannot transfer wounds she did not physically
touch. Transferring to an unwilling target requires sustained contact —
they can break it by moving. If she absorbs too much damage
simultaneously her own circuits begin to rupture. She feels every wound
she transfers in full — the pain does not dampen.

**First Appearance:** Saga 6

**Story Role:** Ally — a healer by nature, a weapon by necessity. Her
ability is the most morally complex of the 11. She has strong opinions
about when wound transfer to an unwilling target is justified.

**PHASITE 4 — KORDAS**

**Ability Name / Type:** Silence Field — the ability to create zones
where no sound exists and no magic can be cast. Within his Silence
Field, mana circuits cannot activate. Spells cannot be initiated or
sustained. Ki is unaffected.

**Visual Signature:** Silence Fields appear as a faint darkening of the
air — like the light has been slightly absorbed. There is no sound
inside them at all. Not even breathing. People inside describe it as the
most unsettling experience of their lives. Kordas himself appears
completely calm inside his own fields.

**Cost & Limits:** The field radius is limited — roughly 30 metres at
maximum strain. Maintaining it costs continuous mana. He cannot cast any
other magic while holding a field. He is vulnerable to Ki-based attacks
inside his own field. If his concentration breaks the field collapses
instantly.

**First Appearance:** Saga 6 — introduced as an enemy, shifts to neutral
by Saga 7

**Story Role:** Antagonist-turned-neutral. He serves the faction
initially — his ability to suppress mana makes him invaluable to Varek
Noss. His allegiance shift is one of Saga 7's key turning points.

**PHASITE 5 — LENNE**

**Ability Name / Type:** True Sight — the ability to see through all
illusions, disguises, magical concealments, and lies. She perceives the
world as it actually is, not as it is presented. She can see a person's
mana channel and circuits as clearly as their face. She can see through
walls if she focuses. She cannot be deceived by any magical means.

**Visual Signature:** Her eyes are permanently different from birth — a
pale, almost colourless grey that seems to look slightly past whatever
she is looking at. When she actively uses True Sight at full intensity
they turn completely white. People find sustained eye contact with her
deeply uncomfortable without knowing why.

**Cost & Limits:** True Sight cannot be turned off. She has perceived
reality without filters her entire life. This is as much a burden as an
ability. Emotional truths — the real feelings behind people's
expressions — are visible to her. She has never been able to pretend she
does not see what she sees. The psychological cost of this is the most
significant limit of her ability.

**First Appearance:** Saga 7

**Story Role:** Ally — crucial in the endgame for identifying faction
infiltrators, navigating political deception, and understanding what
Varek Noss is actually planning. She and Aldric have a complicated
relationship — she can see exactly what he is feeling at all times.

# **SECTION VI — ALDRIC'S POWER PROGRESSION**

Aldric's growth across the series follows three distinct stages. Each
stage corresponds to his mastery of force manipulation, his integration
of sword and magic, and the increasing cost he pays to use both
simultaneously.

## **Stage 1 — Awakening (Sagas 1–2)**

**Force Manipulation State:** Uncontrolled bursts only. He cannot
initiate it deliberately — it activates in moments of extreme emotion or
danger.

**Sword State:** Natural talent. Basic technique. He fights well for his
age but is untrained in any formal style.

**Dual Use:** Impossible. He cannot consciously access both
simultaneously. They surface separately.

**Cost Profile:** Activation causes disorientation, nosebleeds,
temporary circuit stunning. He recovers quickly but has no control over
when it ends.

**Visual:** Silver-white shimmer appears randomly. He cannot sustain or
direct it.

Chapter 3 note — first physical expression: During the preliminary mana
assessment, Aldric's hand moves involuntarily while the pressure
sensation is active — a small, unremarked motion. This is the first
externally physical (though unwitnessed) expression of his Phasite
ability below the threshold of a full uncontrolled burst. He suppresses
awareness of it immediately. The assessor's instrument produces an
anomalous reading during his practical assessment — she checks it twice,
records it without explanation, and moves on. This record exists in the
kingdom's assessment files.

Chapter 6 note — first full awakening:

During the Greyback attack on Ashford, Aldric's force manipulation
activates for the first time at full uncontrolled burst. Trigger: Josse
Vane on the ground, Daran cornered, the Greyback turning back toward
them. No decision made — the ability arrived without permission. The
Greyback is killed outright by the force burst. This is the first
externally visible, witnessed expression of his Phasite ability at full
output.

Visual signature confirmed on-page: silver-white shimmer in the air
around Aldric immediately before activation. Pale white-silver flash at
the point of contact on the Greyback. Brief eye shift from red to
silver, bleeding back to red within seconds. All three elements
witnessed by Daran from inches away.

Physical cost confirmed on-page: nosebleed arriving without Aldric
noticing, disorientation, full-circuit resonance sensation — described
internally as every circuit fired once at full output and still
vibrating, like a bell struck too hard. Recovery was gradual. No
collapse. No unconsciousness. Consistent with Stage 1 cost profile.

Witness: Daran. Distance: inches. His response: silence, one second of
stillness, image filed. He does not have language for what he saw. He
does not need it yet

Chapter 7 note — post-awakening residual state:

In the hours following his first full awakening, Aldric experiences a
low background pressure behind the sternum — described internally as
circuits that fired hard and are still remembering it. Not painful. Not
disorienting. A new baseline sensation, distinct from the pre-awakening
pressure sensation. He ignores it successfully. This is consistent with
the Stage 1 cost profile and does not constitute a new ability — it is
the confirmed aftershock signature of a Stage 1 full-output activation..

## **Stage 2 — Control (Sagas 3–5)**

**Force Manipulation State:** Deliberate activation possible. He can
initiate push/pull and basic kinetic redirection at will. Gravity
manipulation is partial — he can increase gravity but fine control is
unreliable.

**Sword State:** Formally trained. Ki development begins — his circuits
process both mana and Ki simultaneously, which should be impossible. He
is teaching himself.

**Dual Use:** Possible but dangerous. Short windows only — 30 to 90
seconds of simultaneous sword-and-force-magic before circuit strain
becomes critical.

**Cost Profile:** Sustained force manipulation causes chest pain. Dual
use causes circuit burning — visible redness along the circuit pathways
under his skin. Overuse causes bleeding.

**Visual:** Silver-white shimmer is now directional and controlled. His
eyes shift to silver only in high-output moments.

## **Stage 3 — Mastery (Sagas 6–8)**

**Force Manipulation State:** Full command of push/pull, gravity, and
kinetic force within line of sight. He can apply different forces to
multiple targets simultaneously. Gravity manipulation is precise enough
to pin or crush.

**Sword State:** Obsidian-level Ki integration. His blade carries both
Ki sharpness and force acceleration simultaneously — no one has ever
faced a sword strike backed by both.

**Dual Use:** Sustained. He can hold dual activation for several
minutes. But the cost has not disappeared — it has simply become
something he can endure longer before it breaks him.

**Cost Profile:** Extended dual use still causes circuit burning and
bleeding. A full-power activation of everything simultaneously — force
manipulation at maximum range, gravity suppression, Ki blade, all at
once — risks Stage 4 burnout. He has done it once. The recovery took two
weeks.

**Visual:** At full power his eyes are completely silver. The air
distortion around him is visible from a distance. Objects in his
vicinity vibrate slightly from ambient force output. In the final battle
this visual signature is unmistakable on a battlefield.

# **SECTION VII — DRAGONITE MAGIC & VAREK'S TRANSFORMATION**

> *Dragonites do not appear until late in the series. They are treated
> as myth until Saga 6. This section documents what is known — and what
> Varek Noss discovered — for consistency purposes only.*

## **Dragonite Magic — What Is Known**

- Dragonites are the apex race. Their magic does not operate through
  mana channels or circuits as humans understand them.

- Dragonite magic is systemic — it runs through their entire body
  simultaneously, with no separation between storage and expression.
  They do not cast. They are.

- Their output level is categorically beyond the S-class ceiling.
  Standard mana measurement instruments cannot register Dragonite
  signatures accurately.

- They have no known elemental affinity — they express whatever the
  situation requires. This has never been satisfactorily explained by
  human magical theory.

- Physical contact with a Dragonite's active magic is described in
  historical records as experiencing 'the weight of something that has
  always existed.'

## **Varek's Half-Dragonite Transformation**

**How:** Decades of research into Dragonite physiology. Varek identified
a specific biological marker in Dragonite anatomy — a secondary circuit
architecture woven through muscle and bone rather than following the
standard human pathway. He spent years developing a technique to graft
this architecture onto a human body.

**What It Does:** The transformation gives Varek access to a partial
version of systemic Dragonite magic — his mana is no longer stored in a
channel but distributed through his entire body simultaneously. His
output increases by an order of magnitude. His physical durability
becomes inhuman.

**What It Costs:** The transformation is irreversible. It is also
ongoing — his body continues to change after the initial transformation.
By Saga 8 he is no longer fully human in any biological sense. The
psychological cost is implied to be significant but he never
acknowledges it.

**Its Weakness:** Because his magic is now systemic — everywhere at once
— it cannot be focused with the precision a channel-based mage achieves.
He is overwhelmingly powerful but less surgically precise than a skilled
S-class mage. Aldric's force manipulation, which works on physical
forces rather than mana directly, interacts with him differently than
any spell-based attack would.

**The Dragonites' Response:** The actual Dragonites register the
transformation as a violation. Something was taken that was not given.
Their response — ancient, vast, and deliberate — is one of the threads
left open at the series' end.

**SECTION VIII — MONSTER CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM**

There are exactly six formal tiers in the monster classification system
used by hunters, soldiers, and scholars across Valdenmere. The system
runs parallel to the mage rank system but is entirely separate from it.
Below the six formal tiers sits an unofficial, unacknowledged seventh
category that the kingdom does not formally recognise.

Writing rule: Characters reference monster tiers naturally — a soldier
saying 'that's a Feral, we can handle it' versus 'Branded sighting, pull
back and send word' tells the reader everything without explanation.

TIER 1 — FERAL

Natural predators. Large, dangerous to unarmed civilians, killable by a
competent soldier or low-ranked mage. No magical properties. Pack
wolves, giant boars, territorial river creatures, Greybacks. Animal in
intelligence and behaviour. The most common tier encountered by ordinary
settlements.

TIER 2 — MARKED

Animals or creatures with minor mana exposure. Faster, tougher,
occasionally with a single anomalous trait — glowing eyes, unnatural
endurance, hide that resists basic spells. Still animal in intelligence.
Dangerous to small military units. A Marked creature near a village
warrants a kingdom response.

TIER 3 — WARPED

Significant magical corruption. No longer recognisable as their original
species in most cases. Behaviour becomes erratic and occasionally
tactical — not intelligent, but wrong in ways that feel deliberate.
First tier where civilian engagement should never be attempted. A Warped
sighting triggers mandatory military response.

TIER 4 — BRANDED

Origin unclear. These creatures did not evolve — they appeared.
Physically powerful beyond what biology should allow. Some display
rudimentary problem-solving. Kingdom-level response required. Rare
enough that a Branded sighting is a significant event that reaches the
Noble Council's attention.

TIER 5 — SOVEREIGN

The apex of natural monstrosity. Ancient. Territorial over vast areas. A
Sovereign-class creature appearing near a city is a national emergency
requiring Paladin-level response. There are perhaps a dozen confirmed
living Sovereigns in the world. Their territories are mapped and avoided
by treaty in some cases.

TIER 6 — \[UNNAMED\]

Not formally acknowledged by the kingdom. Not in any official
classification document. These are monsters with genuine intelligence —
reasoning, memory, possibly language. The world does not know this tier
exists. A handful of researchers are aware. Varek Noss is among them.
The discovery that some monsters think is a shock reserved for later
sagas. Do not reference this tier explicitly until the author instructs.

THE GREYBACK

Classification: Feral — Tier 1

A large predatory creature native to the northern forests of Valdris.
Bear-mass, low to the ground, broad-shouldered, built for endurance over
speed. Its hide has taken enough scar tissue over a lifetime of
territorial combat that blades do not bite cleanly. Not magical. Not
intelligent. Driven down from the forests when prey is scarce or when
something disturbs its territory. The creature that attacked Ashford in
Arc 2 was a Greyback — directed toward the settlement deliberately by
Drel Voss under Unbound coercion. A Greyback is dangerous to any
undefended settlement. It is killable by a competent soldier or
low-ranked mage. It killed no one in Ashford. It put Josse Vane on the
ground.

THE ETHEREAL CRYSTAL

A ward crystal used by settlements, estates, and institutions to repel
monster approach. Operates by projecting a mana frequency that registers
as threat or wrongness to creature instincts — Feral and Marked tier
creatures will not approach an active crystal's radius. Higher tier
creatures are less affected. The crystal requires regular recharging by
a mage — the cost scales with crystal quality. Cheap crystals exist and
are common in wealthier settlements. High-quality crystals cover larger
radii and hold their charge longer. Ashford has never had one — the
village relied on its distance from the northern forests and could not
sustain the upkeep cost. After the Greyback attack this changes.

Writing note: Ethereal Crystals are background world detail — the
equivalent of a lock on a door. Their absence in poor settlements is a
class detail, not a plot convenience.

BEAST RESONANCE

Classification: Rare mage affinity — Conceptual category

The ability to project mana outward as a frequency that interfaces
directly with a creature's nervous system. At low output: calming
animals, reading emotional states, communicating in impressions rather
than language. At high output: overriding a creature's instincts
entirely — directing it, suppressing fear, pushing it toward a target.
Works on any non-sapient creature. Does not work on beings with full
intelligence. The line between those two categories is something
practitioners think about more than they admit.

Formally assessed as C-class — the ability reads poorly on standard
instruments because of its structural unusualness. In practical terms a
skilled Beast Resonance mage is significantly more dangerous than a
C-rank in specific contexts. In a straight mage duel they are not
exceptional. In a forest with multiple Marked-class creatures at their
direction they are something else entirely.

Currently only one confirmed practitioner: Drel Voss. Whether others
exist is unknown.
