# SAGA 4 — "MORTAR AND STONE" (Year 15)

72 chapters, 6 arcs. Theme: rebuilding trust — what there is to lose. Follows Saga 3's institutional betrayal (Doven's coercion exposed); the friend group and Aldric's trust in the system get rebuilt before Saga 5 tears it down. Aldric remains sword-untrained throughout (Power System Bible Stage 2 — magic-only).
