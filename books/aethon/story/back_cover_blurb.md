Aldric Vane grew up ordinary — a healer's son in a village four days from anywhere that mattered, carrying a pressure under his skin he never let himself examine too closely. Then it surfaced: a shimmer between his fingers that his best friend couldn't look at without flinching.

Now Aldric has passed every exam Greyveil Academy could put in front of him. Daran, the friend who grew up beside him, did not — and has already vanished into the city rather than watch Aldric go on without him.

Greyveil doesn't reward the gifted. It sorts them — by bloodline, by output, by how well they perform the parts of themselves that can be measured. Aldric is walking in alone, carrying something the instructors don't have a name for yet, in a world that has never been kind to students who don't fit the frame.

He's about to find out what the academy does with a pressure it can't explain.
