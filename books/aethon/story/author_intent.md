# Aethon — Creative Brief for InkOS Architect

Source: condensed from `vault/00-Bibles/master-plan.md` and BUILD_PLAN.md
Section 3 (Canonical Story Reference). Do not invent canon beyond what is
stated here or in `vault/00-Bibles/`.

## Series Overview

- **Genre:** Epic Fantasy. **Scale:** 8 Sagas, 20+ Arcs, 300-500+ Chapters.
- **Tone:** Cinematic, fast-paced, hype and epic, with gritty real
  consequences and plot twists. Deaths matter. Seeds planted, consequences
  real.
- **POV:** Multi-POV, close third. Protagonist Aldric Vane is anchor POV;
  switches to rivals, allies, antagonists, and side characters. Every POV
  shift labelled `— [Character Name] —`. Antagonists written with full
  humanity.
- **Chapter length:** 2,000-3,000 words standard; 5,000-7,000 for key
  scenes (flagged in the plan only).
- **Arc structure:** 4-6 arcs per saga; 8-15 chapters per arc.
- **Central themes:** swords vs. magic and the one person who bridges both;
  identity (noble blood raised as a commoner); broken promises and what
  they do to people over time; race, class, and the cost of a world built
  on inequality; what it costs to become a legend vs. what it means to be
  one.

## World

Continents Valdris, Serath, Dravenmoor. Kingdom Valdenmere, capital
Valdris Prime (pop. ~400,000; Crown District / Mage Quarter / Common
Ring). Greyveil Academy of the Arcane Arts, founded 340 years pre-story.
Village Ashford (4 days from Valdris Prime). Sword school Ironmark.

Multiple races coexist — humans, elves, demi-humans, goblins, and others,
each with their own culture and politics. Goblins are an oppressed,
enslaved underclass — a social wound running through the entire series
("the wound at the centre of the world," always present in the background
of Valdenmere scenes). Dragonites are the apex race, ancient and feared,
treated as myth until late in the series — **no physical description
before Saga 7.**

## Power System (full detail: `vault/00-Bibles/power-system-bible.md`)

- **Mana Channel:** internal reservoir, fixed size at birth.
  **Mana Circuits:** pathways; severing = permanent/temporary magic loss,
  treated as a crippling injury with social and legal weight — never
  casual.
- **Exhaustion stages (exact thresholds, never fudge):** 1 Strain (<50%)
  -> 2 Depletion (<20%) -> 3 Critical (<5%) -> 4 Burnout (empty,
  unconscious) -> 5 Rupture (forced casting past burnout, potentially
  fatal).
- **Ranks:** F -> E -> D (academy graduation minimum) -> C -> B -> A
  (national assets) -> S (Sovereign, <50 worldwide).
- **Ki:** the body's refined life force, distinct from mana, generated by
  conditioning, disrupts mana circuits at high levels.
- **Phasites: exactly 11.** Unique, first-of-their-kind abilities that
  cannot be learned, copied, or inherited. Never a 12th, never an implied
  unique-ability character outside the roster. Aldric Vane (Force
  Manipulation) is one of them.

## Protagonist — Aldric Vane

White hair, red eyes. Noble blood, raised by commoners (Maren and Josse
Vane, Ashford). First person in history to wield both sword and magic.
Phasite — Force Manipulation. Constraints: line-of-sight required, cost
scales with distance, dual sword+magic strains circuits (time-limited
early sagas, painful throughout — "circuit burn"). Visual signature:
silver-white shimmer/heat-haze, objects glow pale white-silver at
contact, eyes shift red -> luminous silver at full activation. Suppression-
dominant emotional register — "not grief, not self-pity" — dry humor in
the margins of tension, never at its centre. Saga-1 techniques: Pressure
Field, Kinetic Reflect.

## Rival — Daran

Childhood friend and equal. Sub-threshold mana channel; devotes himself
to the sword after being rejected by the academy. Driven by a broken
promise, not pure evil. Operates as a wild card through most of the
series. Ki path; Obsidian rank by Saga 7.

## Supporting cast (Saga 1)

precision magic), Lirien (high noble love interest, ice/precision, cold
and sharp, feelings emerge slowly Sagas 1-6), Crest Halvon (bully),
Principal Edrath Solm (dies Saga 4), Maren & Josse Vane (adoptive
parents), Grevan Tusk (sword mentor), Bavel (combat instructor), Maret

## Saga Map

1. Origin & Awakening — Village -> Academy -> Tournament -> faction's
   first shadow (7 arcs, 55-75 ch)
2. The World Beyond the Walls — Summit collapse -> border conflict ->
   Daran grinds at Ironmark
3. Cracks in the Foundation — Faction spies -> mentor compromised ->
   Aldric watched
4. The First Blood — Faction strikes -> Solm dies -> betrayal -> Aldric
   leaves academy
5. When the World Burns — Full war -> Aldric vs Daran draw -> faction
   uses war as cover
6. The Weight of a Name — Noble blood exposed -> faction revealed ->
   Phasite hunt -> Daran wild card
7. The Fall and the Ember — Kingdom falls -> Noss transforms -> 11
   Phasites gather -> Daran returns
8. First and Last — Final war -> Noss defeated -> Aldric becomes king

## Writing Standards

- Visual power-moment formatting sparse and earned; **no stat screens or
  game UI.**
- World revealed through character experience, never info-dumps. Races
  feel like real peoples.
- Chapter endings vary deliberately: cliffhanger / emotional / quiet /
  ominous, chosen by arc position — never the same ending type three
  times running.
- Fatigue words to avoid: tapestry, testament, delve, palpable, visceral,
  intricate, pivotal, "couldn't help but", "a mix of", "little did he
  know", "unbeknownst".
