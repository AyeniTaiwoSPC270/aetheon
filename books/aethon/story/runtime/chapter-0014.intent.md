**Review:** Aldric Vane, age 17, lives in Ashford with adoptive parents Maren (healer) and Josse (carpenter). Key hook: the pressure sensation Aldric experiences is not explained—it simply is, and he files it away rather than investigating it.

### Body
## Current task
Aldric must pass through the Greyveil registration turnstile, endure the administrative sorting that separates nobility from commoners, and physically claim a bed in the damp commoner barracks alone.

## What the reader is waiting for right now
1) The reader expects Aldric to enter the prestigious academy and immediately encounter aristocratic contempt while grieving Daran's departure.
2) This chapter partially pays off the transition into the academy environment while widening the social divide, denying Aldric any triumphant entrance and grounding him in cold institutional neglect.

## To pay off / to keep buried
- Pay off: H-02 (Daran's shared ambition promise) → fully resolved as an unfulfilled boyhood pact; Aldric claims a two-person bunk room alone, formalizing their split paths.
- Keep buried: The true meaning of the assessor's sealed note (H-05) → suppress until the mid-arc theory evaluation in Chapter 19.

## What the slow / transitional beats carry
- [Opening gate queue] → Establish the sensory scale of Greyveil's basalt archway, the indentured goblin baggage-handlers clearing noble trunks under whip-crack commands, and Aldric's solitary position without Daran beside him.
- [Registration desk exchange] → Reveal the institutional caste system via bureaucratic routine: parchment quality, assigned wing colors, and the clerk's reaction to Aldric's Ashford dossier. Rynn, Aldric, and the intake officer each run through the sorting moment from their own angle.
- [Corridor walk to the lower sub-levels] → Contrast the sunlit spires of the noble dormitories above with the saltpeter-stained lower tiers reserved for commoners.
- [Barracks interior] → Ground the emotional weight of Daran's absence through the vacant second cot and the cold practicalities of academy survival.

## Three-question check on the key choice
- Protagonist's most important choice this chapter: Aldric refuses to pay an extortionate fee to the intake proctor for a dry upper-tier cot, choosing the damp basement bunk instead.
  - Why this choice? He has minimal coin left after the journey and refuses to compromise his reserves before classes even begin.
  - Does it match current interest? Yes, his priority is conserving funds for food and basic equipment now that he cannot rely on Daran's shared resources.
  - Does it match their persona? Yes, Aldric endures physical discomfort with quiet endurance rather than yielding to casual extortion.
- Antagonist / supporting cast's most important choice this chapter: Rynn steps in beside Aldric in the sorting line, publicly identifying himself with an unsponsored commoner rather than ingratiating himself with the minor noble retainers.
  - Why this choice? Rynn despises noble arrogance and sees in Aldric someone who will not cower or play courtly games.
  - Does it match current interest? Yes, commoners survive Greyveil only in tight mutual pacts; Rynn wants a steady partner.
  - Does it match their persona? Yes, Rynn is combative, pragmatic, and instinctively protective of common-born peers.

## Required end-of-chapter change
- Information change: Aldric learns that commoner students are deliberately sequestered in the sub-level barracks without access to the primary library towers after dusk.
- Relationship change: Aldric and Rynn solidify an uneasy commoner survival alliance.
- Physical change: Aldric is issued his ash-grey novice cloak and bronze intake token, locking him into Greyveil's lowest student rung.
