### Body
## Chapter goal
Aldric commits to the academy assessment as his concrete near-term target

## Current task
Aldric must walk to the spring assessor's office in Ashford and formally request a preliminary review — the first active step he takes toward the academy, converting passive ambition into a committed stake.

## What the reader is waiting for right now
1. The reader has watched two chapters of pressure building — mana flickers, Daran's pamphlet drills, Josse's cryptic line, Maren's unqualified certainty — and expects the next chapter to push Aldric one step closer to the academy gate, with a possible first crack in the Daran-Aldric parity.
2. This chapter gives a partial payoff on the forward momentum (Aldric requests the review, the assessor confirms he has something real) while widening the gap on what exactly his mana anomaly is — the assessor's reaction opens more questions than it closes, and Daran's parallel position is left dangling one beat short of rupture.

## To pay off / to keep buried
- Pay off: H-01 (mana pressure anomaly) → partial only; the assessor witnesses something during the preliminary review — a shimmer, a wrong-color reaction — that confirms the pressure is real and unusual, but gives no explanation; Aldric leaves with a confirmed appointment, not an answer
- Pay off: SP-02 (shared academy ambition) → partial; Daran is present at the assessor's office or arrives immediately after for his own review; both boys are assessed the same morning, establishing the last moment of parity before the divergence; the crack is visible in the assessor's manner but not yet spoken aloud
- Keep buried: H-03 (Josse's precise phrasing and what he knows) → suppress until Arc 1 close or Arc 2; do not let Josse appear or be quoted in a way that forces explanation this chapter
- Keep buried: H-04 (Maren's certainty) → suppress; Maren may appear briefly in a single domestic beat but must not be given a line that demands explanation of her knowledge

## What the slow / transitional beats carry
- [Opening, pre-departure] → Aldric waking and moving through the house in early morning; Maren's workroom already active, smell of dried herbs and lamp oil; Aldric does not eat, pockets something small (the pamphlet page, or a coin — a physical object that anchors the scene); this beat plants the sensory texture of Ashford as something being left, not just lived in
- [Walk to the assessor's office] → the village seen from outside its routines for the first time; Aldric notices the goblin laborer at the mill wheel without commentary — one sentence, no editorializing; this is a world-wound seed, not a theme lecture; the walk's physical detail (gravel, morning light, the sound of the river) grounds the chapter before pressure rises
- [Post-assessment, Daran encounter] → Daran arrives or is already waiting; the two boys exchange a few lines that replay the easy parity of Ch. 1–2, but the assessor's differential reaction (already witnessed by Aldric) now sits underneath the conversation like a weight neither names; Daran's obsessive precision (he has memorized the assessor's reported criteria) contrasts with Aldric's instinctive approach; this beat foreshadows the divergence without announcing it

## Three-question check on the key choice
- Protagonist's most important choice this chapter:
  - Aldric chooses to request the preliminary review himself rather than waiting for Daran to arrange it or for the formal application window — he acts first, alone
  - Why this choice? Josse's line ("you won't be able to set it aside") has been sitting in him since Ch. 2; the sustained pressure event at night made inaction feel dishonest; the request is the only move available to him that is both concrete and private
  - Does it match current interest? Yes — Aldric wants the academy but has been passive; this is the first time desire converts to action
  - Does it match their persona? Yes — suppression-dominant; he does not tell Maren or Daran he is going; he goes alone, early, before the village is fully awake; dry self-sufficiency, not drama
- Antagonist / supporting cast's most important choice this chapter:
  - Daran chooses to arrive at the assessor's office independently, having made the same decision without coordinating with Aldric — parallel initiative, not imitation
  - Why this choice? Daran's obsessive precision means he has already calculated the optimal timing for a preliminary review; his father's contact with the assessor (mentioned Ch. 2) gives him information Aldric lacks; he acts on a plan, not an impulse
  - Does it match current interest? Yes — Daran's entire arc is built on matching Aldric step for step; this is the last chapter where he can still do that
  - Does it match their persona? Yes — Daran is methodical, competitive without malice, and genuinely believes the parallel path will hold; his arrival is not a surprise move, it is the natural output of who he is

## Required end-of-chapter change
- Information change: Aldric now has a confirmed preliminary review date — the academy is no longer an abstraction, it is a scheduled appointment; the assessor's reaction to the shimmer (a pause, a written note, a question asked and then retracted) has been witnessed and filed away by Aldric without being understood
- Relationship change: the Aldric-Daran parity has its first hairline fracture — not spoken, not fought over, but present; the assessor treated their sessions differently and both boys felt it; neither names it; the chapter ends with them walking back toward the village together as if nothing changed, which is the hook
