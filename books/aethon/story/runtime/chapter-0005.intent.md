[Early road — first hour out of Ashford] → Aldric and Daran fall into old walking rhythm; dialogue is light, practical, slightly performative — both boys covering the weight of departure with talk about the exam format or travel time; plants the normalcy that the anomaly will rupture
[Road midpoint — the anomaly beat] → a stumble, a loose stone, a cart passing too close — something physically abrupt; Aldric's hand shoots out and the shimmer happens; an object moves or is stopped by nothing; Daran sees it; neither boy names it; Daran's reaction is the hook — not fear, not awe, something more complicated (recalculation); this is the chapter's only pressure beat and it must land with weight
[Late road — the silence after] → the two boys walk without filling the silence; the conversation that resumes is slightly off-register; Daran does not ask directly; Aldric does not explain; the gap between them is one notch wider than it was this morning, and both of them feel it without saying so; this beat foreshadows the fracture that will widen when Daran is rejected
[Chapter close — first sight of Valdris Prime] → Aldric sees the city for the first time; scale, noise, smell — the Common Ring from the road; the academy is not yet visible or is glimpsed at distance; the chapter ends on Aldric's internal register: not awe exactly, something quieter and more forward-facing; Daran is beside him but the city is already separating them into two different futures

## Three-question check on the key choice
- Protagonist's most important choice this chapter:
  - **Choice:** After the shimmer happens and Daran sees it, Aldric chooses to say nothing — no explanation, no deflection, no joke
  - **Why this choice?** He has no language for it; he has been filing these events away privately since Ch. 1; explaining would require admitting he does not know what it is, which is worse than silence
  - **Does it match current interest?** Yes — Aldric's interest is to get to the academy and not complicate the last hours with Daran before separation; silence is the path of least friction
  - **Does it match their persona?** Yes — suppression-dominant emotional register; dry humor lives at the margins of tension, not at its center; he would not make a joke about this
- Antagonist / supporting cast's most important choice this chapter:
  - **Character:** Daran
  - **Choice:** Daran sees the shimmer, clocks it, and also says nothing — he resumes walking after a beat and changes the subject
  - **Why this choice?** Daran is proud and precise; asking would be admitting he saw something he cannot explain, which puts him at a disadvantage; also, something in him is already doing the math about what Aldric is and what that means for their parity
  - **Does it match current interest?** Yes — Daran's interest is to arrive at the exam on equal footing, not to open a conversation that might confirm a gap he suspects but has not named
  - **Does it match their persona?** Yes — Daran is driven by a broken promise and a need for equivalence; he does not collapse into fear or admiration; he files it and moves on, the same way he filed the assessor's differential reaction in Ch. 3; this is the behavior of someone who will later choose the sword over the academy

Maren and Josse each run through the departure from their own angle this chapter: Maren's goodbye is composed in a way that reads as grief-suppression on first pass but on reread will feel like foreknowledge — she holds Aldric's face, not his shoulders; Josse's farewell is physical and practical, the handshake of a man who trusts the thing he raised; both parents are present at the door but their internal registers are different — Maren is watching something arrive, Josse is watching something leave.

## Required end-of-chapter change
- Information change: Daran now has firsthand witness evidence of Aldric's anomaly — the information exists between them, unspoken, and cannot be unfiled
- Relationship change: the gap between Aldric and Daran is measurably wider; the village-era equilibrium is over; the road has already begun to separate them
- Physical change: Aldric has left Ashford and is at the gates or outskirts of Valdris Prime — the geography of his life has changed permanently
