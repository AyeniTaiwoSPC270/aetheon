## Core_Tags
Commoner-born, fire-adjacent magic, hotheaded but not reckless, loyal before he is strategic — Rynn is the character who says what Aldric won't, which makes him both a liability and a necessary pressure valve in the group dynamic.

## Contrast_Detail
He argues loudly and publicly about everything except the things that actually hurt him, which he mentions once, quietly, and never again.

## Back_Story
Commoner background, came to Greyveil on merit and knows it. Has a chip on his shoulder about the noble-track students that is not unfair given what he has seen, but occasionally misdirects toward the wrong targets.

## Current_State
At Chapter 14, Rynn is newly enrolled at Greyveil. He has not met Aldric yet. He is already in a low-grade argument with someone about dormitory assignments.

## Relationship_Network
Aldric: the alliance that forms because they are both wrong-shaped for the institution in different ways. Lirien: a friction that never fully resolves because they are reading the same room from opposite ends of the class structure.

## Inner_Driver
He wants to prove that the commoner track is not a lesser track, and he wants to do it loudly enough that people cannot pretend not to hear it.

## Growth_Arc
From reactive hothead to someone who has learned when the loud response is correct and when it is the wrong tool — a distinction the war volumes force him to develop.
