## Core_Tags
Minor noble, calm, precision magic, strategic in alliances, genuinely principled — Solen is the character who chose Aldric before it was advantageous and has never fully explained why.

## Contrast_Detail
He is the most measured person in the group and the one most likely to have already thought through the worst-case outcome of any given situation — which he mentions only after the situation has resolved, because he did not want to discourage anyone.

## Back_Story
Minor noble family with enough status to be at Greyveil and not enough to be on the faction's radar. He has watched the high-noble faction politics long enough to find them repellent and the commoner-track resentment understandable. Aldric represents a third option he had not considered.

## Current_State
At Chapter 14, Solen is newly enrolled. He has observed the social landscape of Greyveil's first week with the detachment of someone taking notes, which is exactly what he is doing.

## Relationship_Network
Aldric: the alliance that anchors his Volume 1-4 arc. Lirien: mutual respect across the class divide. Rynn: a friendship that requires active management from both sides.

## Inner_Driver
He wants to be useful to something worth being useful to. He has not yet found it. Aldric is the first candidate.

## Growth_Arc
From detached observer to committed ally — the cost of that commitment surfaces in Volume 4 when the faction strikes and being Aldric's friend becomes genuinely dangerous.
