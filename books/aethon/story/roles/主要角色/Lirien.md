## Core_Tags
High-noble, ice/precision magic specialist, cold at the surface and precise underneath, feelings emerge slowly and then all at once — Lirien is the character who has learned to treat warmth as a liability and is being slowly proven wrong.

## Contrast_Detail
She corrects people's magical theory in the middle of arguments, including her own, when she realizes mid-sentence that she was wrong — a precision so absolute it applies to herself without mercy. She also keeps a small pressed flower from her home estate in the back of her notebook, which she has never explained to anyone.

## Back_Story
High noble family with deep Greyveil ties. Expected to graduate at B-rank minimum and enter a specific institutional track. Aldric is a disruption to the framework she has been given, and her initial coldness is not cruelty but the response of a person who has been taught that disruptions are managed, not engaged.

## Current_State
At Chapter 14, Lirien is already enrolled at Greyveil. She is aware of Aldric's entrance in the way high nobles are aware of anomalies — as a fact to be categorized. She has not yet formed an opinion about him that she considers worth holding.

## Relationship_Network
Aldric: the relationship that restructures her framework across six volumes. Solen: a minor noble peer she respects precisely because he does not perform his minor-noble status.

## Inner_Driver
She wants to be the best at what she does, and she wants that to mean something beyond institutional approval — a desire she has not yet articulated to herself.

## Growth_Arc
From cold categorization to genuine investment across Volumes 1-6, with the war conditions of Volume 5 forcing the acceleration she would not have allowed herself under normal circumstances.
