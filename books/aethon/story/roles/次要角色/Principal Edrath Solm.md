## Core_Tags
Institutional leader, principled within the system's limits, genuinely protective of students, not naive about the faction's existence.

## Contrast_Detail
He has known about the faction's presence inside Greyveil for longer than he has admitted to anyone. He has been managing the exposure, not eliminating it, because he believed management was safer. Volume 4 proves him wrong.

## Current_State
At Chapter 14, Solm is running Greyveil with the particular exhaustion of someone who has been holding a complicated situation together by force of will for a decade. He is aware of Aldric's enrollment and has flagged it internally as requiring careful handling.

## Relationship_to_Protagonist
The institutional authority figure who is neither purely an obstacle nor purely a protector — his death in Volume 4 is the series' first proof that the faction will remove anyone who stands between them and their objective.
