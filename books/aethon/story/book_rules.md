### Power System: Mana Cultivation Stages

**Tier Structure (No Explicit Numbers):**
- **Baseline:** All humans have mana capacity. Baseline students can sense mana but cannot manipulate it. This is the entry state for all first-year students.
- **Stage 1 (Pressure Threshold):** The first breakthrough. The student experiences the "pressure sensation"—a building weight in the chest that spreads outward. When the pressure is released, a minor circuit opens and mana flows through the body for the first time. Physical markers: the student's eyes may change color slightly, their presence becomes more noticeable, they can sense mana in others. Aldric achieves this by the end of Arc 4.
- **Stage 2 (Circuit Stabilization):** The student can open and maintain circuits voluntarily. Mana output increases measurably. Physical markers: aura becomes visible in low light, the student's voice carries more weight, they can sense mana at a distance. This is typically achieved in the second year of academy training.
- **Stage 3 (Resonance):** The student's mana begins to resonate with external sources—other students' mana, environmental mana, artifacts. They can amplify their output through resonance. This is typically achieved in the third year.
- **Master (Tier Transcendence):** The student's mana capacity exceeds the normal range for their age/training. They can manipulate mana in ways that seem to violate the rules of Stage 3. This is rare and typically requires either exceptional talent, rare bloodline, or extraordinary circumstance.

**Progression Rules:**
- Each stage requires a breakthrough moment, not just training accumulation. The breakthrough is triggered by pressure (literal or metaphorical) that forces the student to exceed their current limits.
- Mana output is measurable but not explicitly numbered in the narrative. Instead, it is described through physical sensation, visible effects, and comparative statements ("Rynn's output is higher than Aldric's, but less controlled").
- Power loss is not permitted except through extraordinary justification (severe injury, curse, deliberate sacrifice). Aldric's progression will be monotonic—he will not lose Stage 1 once achieved.
- Tier progression must be earned through effort, sacrifice, or problem-solving. Bloodline advantage exists (noble students often have higher baseline capacity) but does not guarantee advancement.
- Each tier represents a meaningful power difference. A Stage 1 student can defeat a baseline student in direct combat; a Stage 2 student can defeat a Stage 1 student; a Stage 3 student can defeat a Stage 2 student. These are not absolute rules but probabilities.

**Aldric's Anomaly:**
Aldric's white hair and the nature of his mana pressure are not explained in the first 13 chapters. This is intentional. His mana manifests differently than expected—it appears as a shimmer or light rather than as a standard circuit opening. This suggests either (1) a rare bloodline, (2) a curse or blessing from his past, or (3) a fundamental difference in how his mana operates. This will be revealed gradually across the next 100+ chapters.

### Academy Structure

**Module System:**
- **Combat Application:** Physical training, sparring, technique development. Instructor: Bavel.
- **Mana Theory:** Lecture-based instruction on the history, mechanics, and ethics of mana cultivation. Written exams.
- **Circuit Cultivation:** Hands-on practice opening and maintaining circuits. Practical assessments.
- **Practical Integration:** Application of theory and technique to real-world scenarios. Group projects.

**Assessment System:**
- Each module has a mid-year and end-of-year assessment.
- Assessments are ranked (not graded numerically) into tiers: Exceptional, Proficient, Adequate, Remedial.
- Rankings affect module placement in the next year and eligibility for advanced training.
- There is no explicit "pass/fail" for first-year students; all first-year students advance to second year unless they are expelled for disciplinary reasons.

**Social Structure:**
- Noble students (confirmed bloodline, family mana reserves) are assumed to be superior and are given priority in group assignments and mentorship.
- Commoner students (no family mana reserves, self-taught or guild-trained) are assumed to be supplementary and are often assigned to support roles.
- Demi-human students (elf, dwarf, etc.) are rare and are treated as curiosities or threats depending on the noble student's disposition.
- There is no formal caste system, but the informal hierarchy is rigid and enforced through social pressure rather than institutional rule.

### Conflict Structure

**Aldric's Primary Conflicts:**
1. **Internal:** Learning to control his mana pressure, understanding why it manifests differently, managing the pride that comes with advancement.
2. **Social:** Navigating the noble-commoner hierarchy without either submitting to it or directly challenging it. Building alliances with Rynn, Seya, and Kael while maintaining distance from Crest.
3. **Institutional:** Learning the official framework for mana cultivation and reconciling it with his intuitive understanding. Passing module assessments while maintaining his adaptability advantage.
4. **Relational:** Processing Daran's rejection and the fracture in their friendship. Potentially reuniting with Daran and finding a new equilibrium.

**Crest's Escalation:**
Crest's first move (contemptuous comment) failed because Aldric did not care about social positioning. His second move (structural exclusion) will fail because Aldric naturally forms alliances with other outsiders. His third move will be more calculated—possibly involving information control, sabotage of group projects, or leveraging his family's influence to create institutional barriers.
